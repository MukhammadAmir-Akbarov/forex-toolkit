*[SL]: Stop Loss — an order that auto-closes a trade at a preset loss level.
*[TP]: Take Profit — an order that auto-closes a trade at a target profit.
*[RR]: Risk/Reward — ratio of risk to potential profit (1:2 = $2 aimed per $1 risked).
*[R:R]: Risk/Reward ratio (1:2 = you aim for $2 of profit for every $1 risked).
*[RSI]: A 0–100 oscillator measuring overbought / oversold conditions.
*[MACD]: A trend & momentum indicator: difference of two EMAs plus a signal line.
*[EMA]: Exponential Moving Average — recent candles weigh more than older ones.
*[SMA]: Simple Moving Average — the average price over N candles.
*[MA]: Moving Average.
*[ATR]: Average True Range — an indicator of average volatility.
*[ECN]: An account routing orders straight to market: raw spread plus commission.
*[FOMO]: Fear Of Missing Out — pushes you to enter without a valid signal.
*[KYC]: Know Your Customer — broker identity verification (passport, etc.).
*[ESMA]: The European securities and markets regulator.
*[OHLC]: Open-High-Low-Close — the four key prices of a single candle.

*[pip]: The standard unit of price change (usually the 4th decimal; 2nd for JPY).
*[pipette]: 1/10 of a pip (the 5th decimal place).
*[leverage]: Ratio of position size to your own capital (1:100 = $100 controls $10,000).
*[margin]: Money frozen as collateral for an open position.
*[drawdown]: The decline in account balance from its peak.
*[pullback]: A temporary move against the primary trend.
*[breakout]: A price move through a support or resistance level.
*[candlestick]: A price bar showing open, high, low and close (body + wicks).
*[volatility]: The size of price swings over a period.
*[liquidity]: How easily an asset trades without moving the price much.
*[divergence]: Price and indicator disagree — a signal of weakening momentum.
*[backtest]: Testing a strategy on historical data.
