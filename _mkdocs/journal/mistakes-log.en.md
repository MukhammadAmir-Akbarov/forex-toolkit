# Mistakes Log — recurring errors journal

> A dedicated file where you record only **mistakes**, so you can notice what keeps repeating. Re-read it once a month.

## Error Categories

### 1. 🎯 Technical Errors (Analysis)

#### Entry Against the Trend
- **Date:** ___
- **Trade #:** ___
- **What happened:** entered short without noticing that H4 was in an uptrend
- **Why:** did not check the higher timeframe
- **Lesson:** always check D1 + H4 BEFORE entry

#### Weak Signal
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** the candle was not a "hammer" but a "hanging man" (but I couldn't tell the difference)
- **Lesson:** re-read the patterns guide

#### Ignoring a Level
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** entered long right into a strong D1 resistance zone
- **Lesson:** mark D1/H4 levels BEFORE any trade

---

### 2. 💰 Risk Management Errors

#### Risk Too Large
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** took 3% risk on a trade "because I was confident"
- **Lesson:** confidence ≠ edge. Risk 0.5–1% ALWAYS

#### Averaging into a Losing Position
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** added to a position while the first one was in the red
- **Lesson:** NEVER average into a loss

#### No Stop Loss
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** forgot (or intentionally skipped) the SL
- **Lesson:** SL = the first thing I do when opening. No SL — exit immediately

---

### 3. 🧠 Psychological Errors

#### Tilt (Revenge Trading)
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** after a losing trade, immediately opened the next one with increased size
- **Lesson:** 1 loss → 30-minute break. 3 losses → stop for the day

#### FOMO
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** saw a move, jumped in without a signal
- **Lesson:** if the setup was not marked BEFORE the move — skip it

#### Closing in Profit Too Early
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** closed the trade at +0.5R out of fear of a reversal
- **Lesson:** TP is set and does not move

#### Moving the Stop
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** moved SL further from price "to give it a chance"
- **Lesson:** stop = a contract, it does not move. Never

---

### 4. ⏰ Timing and Conditions Errors

#### Trading Into News
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** didn't notice NFP in 30 minutes, opened a trade
- **Lesson:** check the forex factory calendar BEFORE every trade

#### Trading While Tired
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** traded after a sleepless night
- **Lesson:** sleep < 7 h → do not trade

#### Scalping
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** switched to M1 "to get even quickly"
- **Lesson:** my timeframe is H1+. M1 for me = emotions

---

### 5. 📝 Discipline Errors

#### Did Not Log the Trade
- **Date:** ___
- **Lesson:** Journal is mandatory. A trade WITHOUT a record = the trade didn't happen

#### Did Not Use the Checklist
- **Date:** ___
- **Lesson:** printed checklist on the desk, physically tick the boxes

#### Changed Strategy Mid-Series
- **Date:** ___
- **What happened:** after 3 losses started tweaking parameters
- **Lesson:** a series of 3 losses ≠ bad strategy. That's statistics. Do not change parameters on the fly

## Template for a New Mistake

```markdown
#### Brief name of the mistake
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Trade #:** (if from the journal)
- **What happened:** describe the situation in 1-2 sentences
- **Why:** what pushed me in that moment
- **Cost of mistake:** $___ lost / missed
- **Lesson:** what to do next time
- **Future prevention trigger:** what will remind me of this before the next trade
```

---

## Monthly Review

At the end of each month:

1. **Re-read the entire file**
2. **Which mistake repeats most often?**
3. **How much has it cost you?**
4. **What can I do SYSTEMATICALLY so it doesn't repeat?**
   - Not willpower, but a **process**: checklist, broker limit, dedicated trading space...
