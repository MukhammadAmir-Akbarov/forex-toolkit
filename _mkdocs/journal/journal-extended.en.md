## How to use this version

1. Fill it out for every trade during the **first 3 months**.
2. After each session (day/week) — analyse the "Psychology" section.
3. **Find your patterns**: for example, you realise that after bad sleep you lose 80% of your trades → don't trade when tired.

This level of detail matters **only** at the start. After 6 months you can simplify back to the main template.
