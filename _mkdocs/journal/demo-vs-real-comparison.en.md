# Demo vs Real — Comparative Analysis

> When switching from a demo to a live account, results often get WORSE. This file helps you understand **why** that happens in your specific case.

## Why Compare

On a live account:
- 😰 Psychology is completely different
- 💸 Emotions interfere with decisions
- 📊 Spread and slippage are real
- 🐢 Execution may be slower
- 🕰️ Less free time (panic = mistakes)

The goal of comparison: **find what SPECIFICALLY breaks for you** during the transition.

## Analysing the Causes of Divergence

If your live results are worse — ask yourself:

### 🧠 Psychological Causes

- [ ] I am **opening** fewer trades (fear)?
- [ ] I am **closing** trades before TP (fear of giving back profit)?
- [ ] I am **moving stops** further away (hope)?
- [ ] I am **reducing** position size "just in case"?
- [ ] I am **increasing** size "because I'm sure"?
- [ ] I **avoid** good setups after a losing streak?

**If even 1 is yes:** your problem is **discipline**, not strategy.

### 💰 Technical Causes

- [ ] Live spread is wider than the demo server
- [ ] Slippage on entry
- [ ] Slippage on the stop
- [ ] Live quotes can differ from demo quotes
- [ ] Swaps (overnight) reduce long-term trades

**If any apply:** add a field "actual entry price vs planned" to your journal — you will see the difference.

### ⏰ Trading Conditions

- [ ] Less free time → missing setups
- [ ] Trading at an inconvenient time of day
- [ ] Fatigue accumulating
- [ ] Less time for analysis before a trade

## Tactics for Getting Back on Track

### If win rate dropped by 20%+

**Problem:** you are breaking the rules in the moment.

**Solution:**
1. Reduce your live position size by **5×**
2. Physically check off a printed checklist before each trade
3. **Cut** the number of trades to 1–3 per day
4. Record your **emotional state** before each trade (0–10)
5. Return to normal within 2–4 weeks — gradually increase size

### If win rate is close to demo but profit is lower

**Problem:** additional costs.

**Solution:**
1. Calculate how much **spread** eats (often 10–15% of profit)
2. Check if there are **commissions** on your account
3. Account for **swaps** on trades lasting more than 1 day
4. If the difference is > 20% — you may need an ECN account with a tighter spread

### If I am placing smaller positions (fear)

**Problem:** the gap between demo psychology and live psychology.

**Solution:**
1. Start with a **cent account**: $10 = 1000 cents. Psychologically less painful.
2. Once comfortable — a regular account, but position size **5× smaller than calculated**
3. Gradually (by 20% per month) bring it up to the calculated size

## "Going Live" Checklist

Before your first live account, verify:

- [ ] At least **3 months** of consistent profit on demo
- [ ] Win rate consistently ≥ 40% on demo
- [ ] Profit Factor ≥ 1.5 on demo
- [ ] Fewer than 5% of trades with rule violations
- [ ] Journal kept for **every trade** on demo
- [ ] Starting deposit — **$100–$300** (no more)
- [ ] You are **ready to lose this amount entirely**
- [ ] Your livelihood does not depend on this money

If **all** ☑ — you can go live. If anything is **no** — it is too soon.

---

## Psychological Training Before Going Live

Two weeks before the transition:

1. Narrate **every** demo trade out loud: "opening long EUR/USD, stop 25 pips, take 50 pips, risk $5…"
2. Imagine it is **real money**. Does it hurt when the stop gets hit? **Reduce your size.**
3. Open **demo on the platform under the name "live"** — trick your brain
4. Count **how many times a day** you feel the urge to break a rule. The fewer — the more ready you are for live.

---

## Final Thought

Demo is like a **flight simulator on a computer**. Live is like a **real aircraft**. The knowledge is the same, but **the feelings are completely different**. A gradual transition + a strict journal = the only way.
