# Privacy

The site works without an account. Learning progress, answers, journal data and
trade drafts stay in your browser's local storage.

## Anonymous analytics

GoatCounter receives page views and only the names of completed actions:
calculation, Replay, journal import, exam, dashboard recommendation and helpfulness
feedback. Amounts, currency pairs, calculator inputs and journal contents are not sent.

The counter does not use advertising cookies. Browser settings or a content blocker
can disable it completely; every site feature continues to work without analytics.

## External services

Some pages link to external sources. Manually entered exchange rates are not sent
anywhere. If giscus comments are enabled, GitHub's policy applies; comments currently
require a separate owner action.

You can remove local data through the browser's site settings.
