# About the project: who we are and why it's free

> ⚠️ Educational material, not financial advice. Verified: **2026-06-18**.

If this is your first time on this site and you're already on guard — that's
the right instinct. Forex is full of people who want your money. So let's be
honest about who's behind this project and what our interest in it is.

## What this is

**Forex Trading Toolkit** is an open educational project for beginners,
especially from Uzbekistan: a guide, calculators, and Python tools to help
you understand trading **before** you risk money. This is educational
material, **not financial advice**, and not a "buy now" signal.

## Why it's free and how we make money

**We don't. And that's the whole point.**

- **The code is under the MIT license, the content under CC BY 4.0.** It's
  all out in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/MukhammadAmir-Akbarov/forex-toolkit) — you can read it, check it, copy it, edit it.
- **No affiliate links to brokers.** We don't get a commission if you open an
  account with anyone. So we have no reason to push you toward a particular
  broker.
- **We don't sell** signals, "courses," "mentoring," or "VIP access."
- **We don't collect your money** under any pretext.

The project runs as open source: it's built in people's spare time, and it
pays for itself by helping people avoid losing money to scams.

## How we differ from an "Instagram trader"

| "Mentor"/affiliate | This project |
|---|---|
| Promises income, "10% a month" | Shows [why that's a pyramid scheme](uz/trading-vs-deposit.md) |
| Steers you to "their" broker for a commission | Not a single affiliate link |
| Sells signals and courses | Everything free and open |
| Hides the risk | States plainly: 74–89% of beginners lose money |

## How to verify us

Don't take our word for it — **check it yourself**:

- All the code and text are open: [github.com/MukhammadAmir-Akbarov/forex-toolkit](https://github.com/MukhammadAmir-Akbarov/forex-toolkit).
- Found an error or a disputable claim? Open an
  [Issue or Discussion](https://github.com/MukhammadAmir-Akbarov/forex-toolkit/discussions) — we'll fix it.
- The financial calculations in the calculators are covered by tests (also visible in the repository).
