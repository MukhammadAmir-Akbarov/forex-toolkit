# 🎯 LOT — the one thing that will make or break you

!!! abstract "From a real trader's practice (2 years, ~8,000 signals)"
    This chapter is not textbook theory. This is **the most important observation** an experienced trader drew from 2 years of daily gold trading and statistics tracking:

    > **"Pips, SL, TP — all of that is secondary. The main focus must be on the LOT. What makes you or breaks you is the LOT, not the entry point."**

---

## 🚨 Why are 68% of traders in the red?

Real statistics from a channel with thousands of subscribers:

```
22% — in profit (correct risk selection)
68% — in the red (oversized lots)
10% — at zero / just starting out
```

The reason for the 68% is **always the same**: they slowly grind out pennies of profit, and then **one trade with a large lot** wipes out an entire month's results.

!!! danger "Example from practice"
    Deposit $100. A trader opens a **0.1 lot** position on gold (XAUUSD).

    - 1 pip = ~$1 (for 0.1 lot)
    - Stop-loss at 50 pips = **$50 loss** = **50% of the deposit in one trade**
    - 2 stops in a row = deposit cut in half

    **This is not trading. This is a lottery.**

## 📐 Lot sizing rule from a practitioner

The exact formula in his own words:

> **«$100 баланси - 0,01 дан максимал 3-4 та очинг ундан кўп эмас. Мусилмончилик аста секинли билан.»**
>
> *Translation: with a $100 balance — open a maximum of 3–4 positions at 0.01 lot each. Calmly, without rushing.*

### "LOT vs Balance" table (from practice)

| Balance | Max lot per position | Max simultaneous positions | Max total lot |
|---|---|---|---|
| $100 | 0.01 | 3-4 | 0.04 |
| $300 | 0.03 | 3-4 | 0.12 |
| $500 | 0.05 | 3-4 | 0.20 |
| $1,000 | 0.10 | 3-4 | 0.40 |
| $5,000 | 0.50 | 3-4 | 2.00 |
| $10,000 | 1.00 | 3-4 | 4.00 |

!!! warning "This is not «you can go higher». This is the ceiling."
    If you opened a 0.05 lot position on a $100 balance — you are gambling at a casino, not trading.

!!! danger "This table is NOT a substitute for calculating risk from the stop"
    The table has neither a stop-loss nor an instrument — but real risk depends on
    exactly those. Example: 0.01 lot on gold (XAUUSD) with a 150-pip stop means
    approximately **$15** of risk. On a $100 deposit that is **15% per trade**, and 3–4
    positions already mean tens of percent. This **grossly violates** the project rule "risk ≤ 1%".

    Always calculate size from risk and stop, not from balance:
    **risk ($) = lot × stop (pips) × pip value**. Use the
    [position calculator](../tools/position-calculator.md) to do the math. The table above is only
    an upper lot boundary, to be used once the risk-based calculation has already been done.

## 🎯 Why so small?

### 1. Psychology of decisions at different lot sizes

When you are in a position at **2% of your deposit**, you think:
> "Okay, the market moved against me. I'll wait for my stop — it's in place."

When you are in a position at **20% of your deposit**, you think:
> "I can't afford this loss! I'll move my stop right now, it'll bounce back..."

**Large lot → panic decisions → moved stop → disaster.**

### 2. Drawdown must be survivable

A streak of 5 losing trades in a row is **normal** even for a good strategy.

- At lot 0.01 (risk 1%): drawdown = -5% → you survive and keep trading
- At lot 0.05 (risk 5%): drawdown = -25% → psychologically brutal, you start breaking rules
- At lot 0.1 (risk 10%): drawdown = -50% → deposit is nearly wiped out

## ✅ Checklist: "Do I respect the lot?"

Check yourself before every trade:

- [ ] My lot is ≤ 0.01 per every $100 of balance
- [ ] I am opening no more than 3–4 positions simultaneously
- [ ] My potential loss on this trade is ≤ 1% of balance
- [ ] If I lose — it will be unpleasant, but not a catastrophe
- [ ] I am NOT increasing lot size after a losing trade to "get even"
- [ ] I am NOT increasing lot size after a winning trade out of euphoria

!!! danger "If even one item is NO — do not open the trade."
