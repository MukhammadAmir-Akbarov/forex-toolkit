## 6. My Entry Rules

I open a trade **only if ALL of the following are met:**

```
☐  Trend on H4 is clearly defined
☐  Price on H1 is at dynamic support/resistance (EMA50)
☐  Candlestick pattern in the required direction
☐  RSI(14) is not in an extreme zone (40-65 for long, 35-60 for short)
☐  Stop is technically justified (behind the swing + buffer)
☐  R:R ≥ 1:2
☐  Position size calculated using the calculator
☐  No high-impact news in the next 2 hours
☐  I am calm and not "chasing losses"
☐  There have not yet been 3 losses today
```

**If EVEN ONE ☐ is empty — there is NO trade.**

---

## 7. My Exit Rules

- ✅ A trade is closed ONLY by SL or TP
- ✅ After 1R of profit has passed, SL may be moved to breakeven
- ❌ Closing manually before SL/TP is forbidden (except force majeure)
- ❌ Moving SL further from price — forbidden
- ❌ Averaging into a losing position — forbidden

**What counts as force majeure:**
- Carrying over through a weekend / public holiday (close by 22:00 Friday)
- A strong news impulse against your position (if you caught it before 60-70% of the move)
- Technical issues with the broker

---

## 8. Journal and Analysis

- ✅ I record **every** trade (without exception)
- ✅ Minimum fields: date, pair, direction, entry, SL, TP, lot, result, **"did I follow the rules?"**
- ✅ After closing — a comment, emotions, takeaways

**Analysis:**
- Once a **week**: calculate win rate, PF, draw conclusions
- Once a **month**: deep review of the worst trades
- Once a **quarter**: review of strategy and rules

**If the % of rule-compliant trades < 95% for the week** → **pause**, work on discipline.

## 10. Escalation Limits

**When to increase risk/size:**
- ❌ After one good trade
- ❌ After one bad trade
- ✅ After **3 months** of consistent profit
- ✅ Deposit has grown by **50%** from the starting amount
- ✅ Win rate, PF, and DD are stable

**I never increase:**
- % risk per trade (I only increase the dollar amount $$$, not the %)
- Position size within a single trade
- Number of simultaneous positions

## 12. Stop-Trading Limits

**I stop trading (for at least one month) if:**
- ❌ Deposit has fallen 30% from its starting value
- ❌ 3 weeks in a row in the red
- ❌ My emotional state is deteriorating
- ❌ Family / friends notice I am "not myself"

**I stop trading permanently if:**
- ❌ Deposit has fallen 50%
- ❌ The urge to take out a loan to trade has appeared
- ❌ I cannot control my emotions even on demo
