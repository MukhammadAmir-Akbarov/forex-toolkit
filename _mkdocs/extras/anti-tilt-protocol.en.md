# Anti-Tilt Protocol — Emergency Procedure

> **Print this out and keep it somewhere visible.** When you're "losing it" after a loss — you won't remember the rules. You'll remember that YOU NEED TO LOOK AT THE PAPER.

---

## 🚨 ACTIVATE THE PROTOCOL IF:

- [ ] **2 losses in a row** → Level 1
- [ ] **3 losses in a row** → Level 2 (full stop)
- [ ] **Urge to "get even" right now** → Level 3 (no questions)
- [ ] **Opened a trade without the checklist** → Level 3
- [ ] **Moved your stop in the wrong direction** → Level 3
- [ ] **Averaged into a losing position** → Level 3
- [ ] **Heart pounding after a loss** → Level 2
- [ ] **Closed a trade manually outside of plan** → Level 1

---

## 🟡 LEVEL 1 — 30-Minute Pause

### Steps (in this order):

1. **Log the loss in your journal**
   - Not "I'll write it later" — **right now**
   - Field: "emotions" — be honest: "angry / hurt / want to get even"

2. **Step away from the computer**
   - Close MT5
   - Close all chart tabs
   - Leave your phone on the desk

3. **Physical activity**
   - 10 squats, or
   - 20 steps in place, or
   - A walk around the block

4. **Drink water** — a full glass, slowly, in small sips

5. **4-7-8 breathing** (3 times):
   - Inhale for 4 seconds
   - Hold for 7 seconds
   - Exhale for 8 seconds

### After 30 minutes:

- Check how you feel. If **calm** — you may return to the charts, but **with caution**.
- If you're **still angry** — move to Level 2.

---

## 🟠 LEVEL 2 — Full Stop for the Day

### Steps:

1. **Close ALL open positions** (if not critical to your strategy)
   - If there are pending orders — delete them

2. **Close MT5 completely**

3. **Write in your journal**:
   ```
   Date: ___
   State: tilt after ___ losses
   Action: stopping for the day
   Emotions (1-10): ___
   ```

4. **Mute Telegram signals** for today

5. **Don't open any charts** until tomorrow. **NOT ONE.** Not even "just to take a look."

6. **Plan something enjoyable** for the rest of the day
   - A walk
   - A movie
   - Meeting friends
   - Anything NOT related to trading

7. **In bed by 23:00.** No charts before sleep.

### Tomorrow:

- Before opening MT5 — re-read this protocol
- Re-read `extras/trading-plan-template.md`, especially the "Psychological Rules" section
- If you feel **ready** — start with **half your normal position size** on the first trade
- If you're **still shaky** — move to Level 3

---

## 🔴 LEVEL 3 — One Week Without Charts

### When it activates:

- Levels 1 and 2 didn't help
- A series of losses has turned into a large drawdown (>10% of deposit)
- Family / loved ones notice changes in you
- Thoughts of borrowing money to get even have appeared
- You noticed that you're breaking rules deliberately

### Steps:

#### Day 1:
1. **Log out of MT5 completely**, delete the app from your phone
2. **Block your access** to the broker's website (e.g., via Cold Turkey, freedom.to)
3. **Tell someone close**: "I'm not trading for a week. If you see me at the charts — remind me."
4. **Write down the loss** as a story: date, amount, what led up to it, what you feel right now

#### Days 2–3:
- No charts
- No trading Telegram channels
- No YouTube "how to make money" videos
- Sport, reading something NOT about trading, socialising

#### Days 4–5:
- You may re-read `extras/psychology.md` once more
- Re-read your Trading Plan
- **Theory only**, no charts

#### Days 6–7:
- Analyse your last 10 trades (without opening charts)
- What do the losing trades have in common?
- What do the winning trades have in common?
- **Write a letter to yourself** about what you've learned

### Returning:

- **One more week on demo** with **a quarter** of your normal position size
- Every day — review your emotions
- If you slip again — seek professional help (psychologist)

---

## 🆘 LEVEL 4 — One Month or More

### When:

- Drawdown > 30% in a week
- Thoughts of taking a loan / borrowing money have appeared
- Impact on family / work / health
- You can't manage even Level 3

### Steps:

1. **Stop trading completely** — for at least 30 days
2. **Close your live account** (or withdraw funds to a safe place)
3. **Find a focus OUTSIDE trading** for this period
4. **Talk to a professional** — psychologist, life coach
5. After 30 days — **honest self-assessment**: do I actually want to trade?

### Signs that you should stop for good:

- Impossible to get through a day without the charts
- Dreaming about trading
- Sacrificing sleep / family / health
- Taking on debt
- Hiding losses from loved ones

**Trading is a profession, not a lottery. If it's breaking you — walk away.**

## 📋 DAILY PREVENTION PROTOCOL

To avoid reaching the emergency levels:

### Morning
- [ ] Slept ≥ 7 hours?
- [ ] Not hungry?
- [ ] Emotional baseline calm (1-10): ____ (if <6 — don't trade)
- [ ] Re-read your entry rules?

### Before each trade
- [ ] Checklist physically on the desk
- [ ] Position calculator open
- [ ] Journal open for entry
- [ ] Urge to "get even" = 0?

### After each trade
- [ ] Entry in journal **immediately**
- [ ] If a loss — deep breath in, breath out. 5-minute pause.
- [ ] If a win — no euphoria, don't increase risk

### Evening
- [ ] Closed MT5
- [ ] Logged today's result
- [ ] Not opening charts after 22:00

## 🧘 Anchor Phrases

When you feel the urge to break the rules — say these aloud:

> **"This is just one trade out of a thousand in my career."**

> **"The market doesn't know I exist. It is not 'against' me."**

> **"Discipline beats intuition."**

> **"I work with probabilities, not certainties."**

> **"It's better to miss a good trade than to take a bad one."**
