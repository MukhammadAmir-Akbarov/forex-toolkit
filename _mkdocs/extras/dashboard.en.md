# 🧭 Student Dashboard

!!! abstract "Why this page exists"
    The dashboard brings your learning path into one place: completed lessons,
    the final exam, the Replay trainer and the web journal. Everything is stored
    **only in your browser** (`localStorage`) and is never sent to a server.

!!! warning "Educational material, not financial advice"
    The dashboard helps you choose the next learning step. It does not judge live
    money readiness and does not guarantee profit.

## How to use it

1. Mark pages as completed with the **Mark as read** button.
2. Pass the final exam with at least 80%.
3. Complete a few episodes in the Replay trainer.
4. Upload a CSV to the web journal to see discipline and results.
5. Come back here: the dashboard will suggest your next step.

## Backup

**Export JSON** saves the full journal, trade plans and drafts, progress, exam,
Replay, and tool settings. Import first shows the backup contents and version;
nothing is overwritten until you confirm the restore.
