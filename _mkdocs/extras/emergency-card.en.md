## 🔴 IF I WANT TO TAKE A LOAN TO "WIN IT BACK"

> **STOP. This is the most dangerous signal. Do not do it.**

### What to do:

```
1. Delete the broker's app from your phone RIGHT NOW
2. Block the broker's site in /etc/hosts:
     sudo nano /etc/hosts
     add:
     127.0.0.1   broker-name.com
3. Hand your cards to a trusted person for 7 days
4. Call a specialist (see below)
```

## 🚫 WHAT NOT TO DO IN A CRISIS

```
❌ Take a loan / borrow money
❌ Ask parents / friends for money
❌ Sell assets (car, equipment)
❌ Withdraw money from savings / deposits
❌ Accept a "small" broker bonus with a wagering requirement
❌ Trust anyone who promises to "recover your money" for a fee
❌ Join "exclusive" VIP channels with big promises
```

---

## ✅ WHAT TO DO IN A CRISIS

```
✅ Close the platform
✅ Call someone from the list above
✅ Leave the house for 1–2 hours
✅ Go to bed early
✅ Make decisions tomorrow only with a clear head
✅ Accept the loss as part of the learning process
```
