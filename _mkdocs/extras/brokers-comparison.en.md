## How to Check a Broker in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: License
1. Go to the regulator's website:
   - **FCA UK:** [register.fca.org.uk](https://register.fca.org.uk)
   - **CySEC:** [cysec.gov.cy/en-GB/entities/investment-firms/cypriot/](https://www.cysec.gov.cy/en-GB/entities/investment-firms/cypriot/)
   - **ASIC:** [asic.gov.au](https://asic.gov.au)
2. Search for the broker by name
3. Check whether the **license is active** and **what services it covers**

### Step 2: Reviews
1. **ForexPeaceArmy.com** — search for the broker, look at reviews from the last 6 months
2. **TrustPilot** — filter by year
3. **Smart-Lab** and **mql5.com** — Russian-language reviews

### Step 3: Real Test
1. Open a **demo account** (no money required) → try the interface
2. Contact **support** — ask a question, check the response time
3. **Do not deposit a large amount right away** — start with $100–$300 on a live account

### Step 4: Withdrawal
1. After funding, **immediately try to withdraw** $20–$30
2. If the withdrawal goes through in 1–3 days without issues → the broker can be trusted
3. If they delayed it or asked for unusual documents → close the account

## Red Flags — Do NOT Open an Account

🚩 "Guaranteed profit of 30% per month"
🚩 "EUR is at a record high right now — deposit and get a 100% bonus!"
🚩 They call and pressure you to increase your deposit
🚩 "A personal manager will trade on your behalf"
🚩 No license anywhere, or only in Vanuatu / SVG
🚩 The site has been running for 2 years and there is nothing about the company on Google
🚩 A bonus that requires trading 30 lots in one week
🚩 Deposit withdrawal is blocked until the bonus wagering requirement is met
🚩 Support only responds during business hours on weekdays (once a week)

## Typical Fraud Schemes

### 1. Bottom-level "Forex Investment" Schemes
You are promised training in trading in exchange for a share of the profit. In reality:
- They ask you to transfer funds to their "account"
- They show you "successful trades" (photoshopped)
- After a month, all contact is cut off

### 2. Fake Platforms
You are given an interface that **looks like MT5**, but it is a **simulator**. The money has gone to fraudsters; the quotes are fake.

### 3. "Exclusive Signal Channel on Telegram"
You pay $200/month for signals. Out of 100 subscribers:
- 30 "enter profitably" — they receive one set of signals
- 30 "enter at a loss" — they receive a different set
- After a month, 30 praise the channel, 30 leave, the rest say nothing
- The channel looks profitable, **but this is a statistical illusion**

### 4. PAMM Accounts with a Guarantee
"Trust your money to a manager." After 3 months:
- 70% of the capital is gone
- "The market fell, it is not our fault"
- You will not get your money back

## If You Have Already Been Scammed

1. **Stop all transfers immediately**
2. Collect **all documents** (statements, screenshots, correspondence)
3. **File a complaint** with the regulator (if the broker is regulated)
4. **File a report with local law enforcement** (if there is a criminal case)
5. On a crypto exchange (if money was sent via USDT) — freeze your wallet and contact their support
6. **Do not pay "money recovery specialists"** — this is a second wave of fraud
