## 1. What Forex Is and Realistic Expectations

**Forex** (Foreign Exchange, FX) is the international currency exchange market. It is the largest financial market in the world, with a daily turnover of **over $7.5 trillion** (BIS data, 2022).

### Who Trades on Forex
- **Central banks** — manage the exchange rate of the national currency
- **Commercial banks** — the primary market makers
- **Hedge funds and large corporations** — hedge currency risk
- **Retail traders (you)** — speculate through brokers. This is **less than 5%** of total market volume.

### How People Make Money on Forex
You don't buy "currency" in any physical sense. You trade **currency pairs** — betting that one currency will rise relative to another.

- **Long (buy)** — you bet that the pair's price will rise
- **Short (sell)** — you bet that the pair's price will fall

Example: if you think the euro will strengthen against the dollar, you open a long on EUR/USD. If you think the euro will weaken, you go short.

### Honest Statistics — Read VERY Carefully

| Source | Statistic |
|---|---|
| ESMA (Europe) | 74–89% of retail CFD traders lose money |
| NFA (USA) | The average lifespan of a retail forex account is 4 months |
| Broker studies | Only 10–15% of retail traders are consistently profitable after 1+ year |

**Forex is NOT:**
- ❌ a way to "get rich quick"
- ❌ passive income
- ❌ a replacement for your job in a couple of months
- ❌ something you can learn from TikTok videos

**Forex IS:**
- ✅ a profession with a learning curve of **1–3 years** before you reach consistency
- ✅ work with probabilities, not certainties
- ✅ discipline and psychology matter more than any "secret strategy"

### Forex ≠ Investing

| | Investing (stocks, ETFs) | Forex Trading |
|---|---|---|
| Time horizon | Years to decades | Minutes to days |
| Leverage | Usually none | Up to 1:500 and higher |
| Source of profit | Business growth, dividends | Price fluctuations |
| Risk of ruin | Low with diversification | High if you make mistakes |
| Suitable for beginners | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ Only after extensive training |

If your goal is to **build capital**, start with diversified ETFs and long-term investing. Forex is active speculation, and you should first understand whether you are ready for that lifestyle.

## 2. Basic Terminology

### Currency Pairs

Written in the format `BASE/QUOTE`, for example `EUR/USD`. The price shows **how many units of the quote currency (USD) you need to buy 1 unit of the base currency (EUR)**.

`EUR/USD = 1.0850` means: 1 euro = 1.0850 dollars.

**Groups of pairs:**

- **Majors** — the most liquid, with tight spreads:
  EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NZD/USD
- **Minors (crosses)** — pairs without USD: EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY
- **Exotics** — a currency from an emerging market: USD/TRY, USD/ZAR, USD/MXN. Wide spreads, high volatility — **beginners should not trade these**.

### Pip, Point, Tick

- **Pip** — the standard unit of price change. For most pairs it is the 4th decimal place. EUR/USD moving from 1.0850 to 1.0851 = +1 pip.
- For JPY pairs, a pip is the 2nd decimal: USD/JPY 150.25 → 150.26 = +1 pip.
- **Point (pipette)** — 1/10 of a pip (the 5th decimal). Modern brokers display prices with 5 decimal places.

### Lot — Position Size

| Lot Type | Volume of Base Currency | Value of 1 Pip (EUR/USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (1.0 lot) | 100,000 | ~$10 |
| Mini (0.1 lot) | 10,000 | ~$1 |
| Micro (0.01 lot) | 1,000 | ~$0.10 |
| Nano (0.001 lot) | 100 | ~$0.01 |

**For beginners — only micro-lots (0.01)**, or nano if the broker offers them.

### Spread and Commission

- **Bid** — the price at which you can sell
- **Ask** — the price at which you can buy (always higher than Bid)
- **Spread** = Ask − Bid. This is the broker's fee on every trade.

Example: EUR/USD Bid 1.08500, Ask 1.08512 → spread = 1.2 pips.

Some brokers charge a commission instead of or in addition to the spread (ECN accounts): for example, $7 per standard lot.

### Swap (overnight)

If you leave a position open overnight, a **swap** is credited or debited — the difference between the interest rates of the two currencies in the pair. It can be positive or negative. For a beginner, the key point is: **intraday trading has no swaps**, but a long-held position on the "wrong" side will gradually be eaten away.

### Leverage and Margin

**Leverage** lets you open a position larger than your deposit.

- 1:30 leverage → a $100 deposit lets you open a $3,000 position
- 1:100 leverage → $100 = a $10,000 position
- 1:500 leverage → $100 = a $50,000 position

**Margin** is the amount that gets "locked up" to support an open position.

> **⚠️ Leverage is a double-edged sword.** It magnifies BOTH profit AND loss by the same factor. At 1:500 leverage, a move of just 0.2% against you wipes out your deposit.
>
> **Beginner's rule: leverage no higher than 1:10–1:30**, even if the broker offers 1:500.

### Long / Short

- **Long (buy)** — opened at 1.0850, waiting for a rise, will close at a higher price → profit
- **Short (sell)** — opened at 1.0850, waiting for a fall, will close at a lower price → profit

### Stop Loss / Take Profit

- **Stop Loss (SL)** — an order that automatically closes the position at a defined loss. **MUST be placed on every single trade.**
- **Take Profit (TP)** — an order that automatically closes the position once the target profit is reached.

### Margin Call and Stop Out

- **Margin call** — the broker's warning: "your free margin is running out, we will soon force-close positions"
- **Stop out** — the broker force-closes positions (usually when the margin level drops to 20–50%). This means **you've lost almost your entire deposit**.

## 3. How a Trade Works — A Concrete Example

**Scenario:** $1,000 deposit, 1:30 leverage, trading EUR/USD.

### Step 1. Analysis
You believe EUR/USD will rise from 1.0850 to 1.0900 (50 pips).

### Step 2. Position Sizing
- You're willing to risk 1% of your deposit = **$10**
- You place a stop-loss 25 pips below entry = 1.0825
- Position size = risk / (stop in pips × pip value)
- 0.04 lots → value of 1 pip = $0.40 → 25 pips × $0.40 = $10 ✓

In the terminal you enter **0.04 lots** (or the closest available — for example, 0.04 micro).

### Step 3. Opening the Position
- Buy EUR/USD 0.04 lot at the Ask price 1.08512
- Stop Loss: 1.0825
- Take Profit: 1.0900 (risk/reward = 1:2)

The broker locks up margin: (0.04 × 100,000 × 1.0850) / 30 = ~$145.

### Step 4a. The Trade Moves Into Profit
Price reaches 1.0900, TP triggers:
- Profit = (1.0900 − 1.08512) × 0.04 × 100,000 ≈ **$19.50**
- Minus the spread ≈ $0.50
- **Net profit ≈ $19**

### Step 4b. The Trade Moves Into Loss
Price falls to 1.0825, SL triggers:
- Loss = (1.08512 − 1.0825) × 0.04 × 100,000 ≈ **−$10.50**
- Deposit: $989.50

### The Key Takeaway
A stop-loss **caps your loss at a known amount in advance**. Without it, one sharp market move can wipe out your entire deposit. **Never trade without a stop-loss.**

## 4. Choosing a Broker

This is one of the most important steps. A bad broker = you lose money regardless of your strategy.

### The Main Rule: Only Regulated Brokers

A regulator forces the broker to follow rules: segregated client accounts, transparent execution, compensation funds.

**Serious regulators:**
- 🇬🇧 **FCA** (UK) — the strictest
- 🇨🇾 **CySEC** (Cyprus, EU)
- 🇦🇺 **ASIC** (Australia)
- 🇺🇸 **NFA / CFTC** (USA)
- 🇨🇭 **FINMA** (Switzerland)

**Weak / offshore regulators** (at your own risk): SVG, Vanuatu, Belize, Seychelles — these provide almost no protection. Many brokers register an offshore branch specifically for clients from countries like yours in order to offer high leverage. Understand that there is essentially no legal protection in such cases.

### What to Look At When Choosing

1. **License** — verify it on the regulator's website (FCA: register.fca.org.uk; CySEC: cysec.gov.cy)
2. **Account type** — Standard / ECN / Pro. A beginner doesn't need ECN — start with Standard or a Cent account.
3. **Minimum deposit** — $10–$100 for a cent account, $100–$500 for standard
4. **Spreads** — EUR/USD should be 0.5 to 2 pips during liquid hours
5. **Execution** — Market Execution is preferable to Instant Execution
6. **Deposits/withdrawals** — must include methods convenient for you (card, USDT, bank)
7. **Reviews** — but be careful: many reviews, both positive and negative, are paid. Check ForexPeaceArmy, Trustpilot, and major forums — look for **recurring** complaints, especially about withdrawals.
8. **Time in business** — at least 5–10 years on the market
9. **Support** — preferably in your language, with fast response times

### Regional Considerations for Your Country

- Most global brokers accept clients from your country
- Convenient deposit methods: **USDT (TRC-20)**, sometimes Visa/Mastercard in USD
- Local currency cards usually work poorly — better to have a USD card or a USDT wallet
- Local-language support — most major brokers have it
- Currency trading is regulated by local legislation — see section 11

### Well-Known Regulated Brokers (for reference, not an endorsement)

As examples of seriously regulated brokers that work with international clients, you can look into: IC Markets, Pepperstone, FP Markets, Tickmill, Exness, FxPro, OANDA, Saxo Bank. **This is not a recommendation** — you should compare conditions and regulation yourself and read recent reviews.

### Red Flags — DO NOT Open an Account with a Broker That:

- 🚩 Promises "guaranteed profits" or "30% per month returns"
- 🚩 Calls you and pressures you to deposit urgently
- 🚩 Has no license from a serious regulator
- 🚩 Offers a "personal manager" who will trade for you
- 🚩 Gives huge bonuses (50–100% on top of your deposit) with abusive wagering conditions
- 🚩 Has withdrawal problems mentioned in reviews
- 🚩 Has been around for less than 2–3 years
- 🚩 Is registered only offshore, with no serious license

## 5. Trading Platforms

### MetaTrader 4 (MT4)
- The classic, supported by almost every broker
- Simple and lightweight
- Huge library of expert advisors (EAs) and indicators
- Downsides: outdated interface, strategy tester is weaker than MT5

### MetaTrader 5 (MT5)
- The modern successor to MT4

- Not compatible with all old MT4 scripts

### cTrader
- Clean interface, transparent ECN
- Fewer brokers support it

### TradingView
- The industry's best web-based tool for **chart analysis**
- You can connect some brokers and trade directly from the chart
- The free version is sufficient to start

### Broker Mobile Apps
- Useful for monitoring
- **Do not trade from your phone on emotion** — misclicks and distractions

1. Sign up with a broker → personal cabinet

3. Download the platform (for example, MT5) from the broker's website

5. Open a EUR/USD chart → learn the interface

## 6. Demo Account — A Mandatory Stage

**A demo account is a free account with virtual money** that runs on real market quotes.

### At Least 2–3 Months of Demo-Only Trading

No real money until:
- ✅ You can use the platform reliably without errors
- ✅ You have tested your strategy on at least 50–100 trades
- ✅ You always place an SL/TP
- ✅ You understand your own psychology (how you react to losing streaks)
- ✅ You have a stable positive equity curve over 3 months

### Trading Journal

**Without a journal you will NOT learn.** Record every trade. Minimum fields:

| Date | Pair | Direction | Size | Entry | SL | TP | Reason for entry | Result ($) | Emotions/Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-13 | EUR/USD | Long | 0.01 | 1.0852 | 1.0825 | 1.0900 | Pullback to MA200 on H1 | +$5 | Calm, followed the plan |

At the end of the week — review: which trades followed the plan? Which were emotional? What patterns repeat?

### When to Move to a Live Account

- ✅ ≥ 3 months of consistently profitable demo trading
- ✅ Clear entry/exit rules written down
- ✅ A trading journal that is actually kept
- ✅ Risk management rules never broken, not even once
- ⚠️ And a **minimum live deposit** — $100–$300, no more. Psychology changes on a live account: even if you were profitable on demo, the first weeks on real money are a new chapter.

## 7. Risk Management — The Most Important Section

Re-read this section three times. It is more important than any "strategy".

### Rule #1: No More Than 1% Risk Per Trade

**Risk per trade ≤ 1% of your deposit.** For beginners — **0.5%**.

| Deposit | 1% risk | 0.5% risk |
|---|---|---|
| $100 | $1 | $0.50 |
| $500 | $5 | $2.50 |
| $1,000 | $10 | $5 |
| $10,000 | $100 | $50 |

### Position Sizing Formula

```
Position size (lots) = (Deposit × Risk%) / (Stop in pips × Pip value per 1 lot)
```

Example: $1,000 deposit, 1% risk = $10, 30-pip stop on EUR/USD (1 lot = $10/pip):

```
Size = $10 / (30 × $10) = 0.033 lots
```

You round to 0.03 — that's your position.

### Rule #2: Risk/Reward Ratio (R:R) ≥ 1:2

For every $1 of risk, aim for at least $2 of potential profit.

Why this is critical: even if you are right only 50% of the time, at R:R = 1:2 you are still profitable.

| Win rate | R:R 1:1 | R:R 1:2 | R:R 1:3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30% | −40% | **−10%** | **+20%** |
| 40% | −20% | **+20%** | **+60%** |
| 50% | 0% | **+50%** | **+100%** |
| 60% | +20% | **+80%** | **+140%** |

(approximate, excluding spread and commission)

### Rule #3: Daily and Weekly Loss Limits

- **3 losing trades in a row** → stop for the day
- **−3% of deposit in a day** → shut down the terminal
- **−6% in a week** → pause until the next week

This saves you from **tilt** — the state where, after a loss, you try to "get even" and lose everything.

### Rule #4: The Math of Ruin

Recovering from a large drawdown is **much harder** than avoiding it in the first place:

| Loss | Required Recovery |
|---|---|
| −10% | +11% |
| −25% | +33% |
| −50% | **+100%** |
| −75% | **+300%** |
| −90% | **+900%** |

That's why a small drawdown is survivable, while a big one is almost a death sentence.

### Rule #5: Beginner Leverage ≤ 1:30

Even if the broker offers 1:500. Your effective leverage (position size relative to deposit) should be even lower — typically **1:3 to 1:10**. High available leverage is just an option, not a reason to use it.

### Psychology: Your Biggest Enemy Is Yourself

Common traps:
- **Greed** — increasing risk after a win
- **Fear** — closing profitable trades too early, holding losing trades too long
- **Tilt** — after a loss, trying to win it back with increased risk
- **FOMO** (fear of missing out) — entering a trade because "it's already moving", without a signal
- **Confirmation bias** — looking for reasons to keep a position instead of admitting you're wrong

The cure: rules written down BEFORE you open a trade, plus the journal.

## 8. Market Analysis

There are two main schools. Most traders combine them.

### Technical Analysis (TA)

Studies the **price chart**, based on the idea that all information is already priced in.

**Candlesticks (Japanese candlesticks):**
- Body — between the open and close prices
- Wicks (shadows) — the high and low of the period
- Green/white — price went up; red/black — price went down

**Core concepts:**
- **Trend** — the direction of movement (uptrend / downtrend / sideways)
- **Support** — a level where price bounces upward
- **Resistance** — a level where price bounces downward
- **Breakout** — price breaking through a level
- **Pullback (retracement)** — a temporary move against the trend

**Popular indicators:**

| Indicator | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Moving Average (MA / EMA)** | Smoothed price — trend and dynamic support/resistance |
| **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** | Overbought/oversold conditions (>70 / <30) |
| **MACD** | Trend strength and change |
| **Bollinger Bands** | Volatility and price extremes |
| **ATR (Average True Range)** | Average candle size — helps with stop placement |

**Beginner's tip:** don't pile on 5 indicators. Start with price + one moving average (for example, EMA 50 or 200) + support/resistance. That's enough for your first year.

### Fundamental Analysis (FA)

Studies the **macroeconomic factors** that move currencies.

**What matters:**
- **Central bank decisions** on interest rates (FOMC, ECB, BoE, BoJ, RBA, BoC). The higher the rate, the stronger the currency (roughly).
- **Inflation** (CPI, PPI)
- **Unemployment**, especially **NFP** (Non-Farm Payrolls in the USA, the first Friday of the month)
- **GDP**
- **Geopolitics** — wars, sanctions, elections

**Where to look:**
- [Forex Factory](https://www.forexfactory.com/) — the best economic calendar, free
- [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/) — calendar + news
- The calendar inside your broker's terminal

**Beginner's rule:** in the **30 minutes before and after** a high-impact news release (NFP, Fed decision) — **do not trade**. Volatility blows out stops.

### Which School to Choose?

To start — **technical analysis on H1/H4 + avoiding trades during high-impact news**. Pure fundamentals are too complex for a beginner, and pure technicals without macro context are risky.

## 9. A Simple Starting Strategy

> This is a **teaching example**, not a signal and not a "holy grail". The goal is to show you the structure of a strategy that you will refine yourself through journaling and testing.

**"Pullback to EMA50 With the Trend" — for H1 / H4**

### Entry Conditions (Long)
1. H4 timeframe — price is above EMA200 (uptrend)
2. On H1 — price has pulled back to EMA50 and forms a bullish candle (hammer, engulfing)
3. RSI(14) on H1 > 40 and not overbought
4. No major news events in the next 2 hours

### Entry Conditions (Short)
1. Price below EMA200 on H4 (downtrend)
2. On H1 — price has pulled back up to EMA50, forms a bearish candle
3. RSI(14) < 60
4. No major news events in the next 2 hours

### Trade Management
- **Stop Loss:** beyond the last swing high (for short) / swing low (for long) + 5–10 pips
- **Take Profit:** at least **2 × the distance to the stop** (R:R = 1:2)
- **Position size:** using the formula from section 7 — 0.5–1% deposit risk
- **Working timeframe:** H1 for entry, H4 for trend direction. **Don't drop down to M1/M5** — that's noise and stress.

### Pre-Trade Checklist

```
☐ Trend on H4 in my direction?
☐ Signal on H1 (pullback to EMA50 + candle) present?
☐ RSI not at extremes?
☐ Stop-loss calculated?
☐ Take-profit ≥ 2× stop?
☐ Position size matches 0.5–1% risk?
☐ No major news in the next 2 hours?
☐ Am I calm, not trying to "win it back"?
```

**If even one ☐ is unchecked — do not open the trade.**

## 10. Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake | What Happens | How to Avoid It |
|---|---|---|
| **Trading without a stop-loss** | One sharp move wipes out the deposit | SL on every trade, set at the moment of opening |
| **Averaging down on a losing position** | "I'll add more and the average price will be better" — usually leads to a blown account | Forbid yourself. Loss = close, do not add |
| **Too much leverage** | A tiny move against you = margin call | Leverage ≤ 1:30, effective leverage 1:3–1:10 |
| **Trading on emotion** | After a loss you want revenge → more losses | The "3 losses → stop for the day" rule |
| **Moving to live too early** | Demo ≠ live. On a live account, fear and greed are different | At least 3 months of profitable demo trading |
| **No trading journal** | You can't see your patterns and you repeat mistakes | Every trade goes in the journal |
| **Signals from Telegram channels** | You pay for someone else's losses and never learn yourself | Ignore "guru" channels entirely |
| **"I know where the market is going"** | Certainty kills | Work with probabilities, not predictions |
| **Trading with borrowed money** | Psychological pressure → disaster | Only spare money you can afford to lose |
| **Too many pairs** | You can't keep up, you get confused | Start with 1–2 pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD) |
| **Scalping on M1/M5** | Huge stress, the spread eats your profit | Minimum H1, ideally H4 |
| **Trading during news** | Spreads widen, stops get triggered | 30 min before and after major news — pause |

## 11. Legal and Tax Aspects (Uzbekistan)

> **Disclaimer:** this is general information. Legislation changes. **Consult a tax advisor or lawyer** before trading with real money.

### Status of Forex Trading in Uzbekistan

- Currency trading activity is governed by Uzbek legislation and Central Bank regulations
- Retail trading with foreign brokers is not prohibited for individuals
- An individual's trading income is treated as income that is **subject to declaration**

### Taxes
- In general, an individual's income is subject to personal income tax (PIT). **Check current rates and the declaration procedure** on the Tax Committee of Uzbekistan website (soliq.uz) or with a consultant.
- Count your **net annual result** (profits minus losses), not each individual trade

### What to Keep
- ✅ Broker statements for each month and year
- ✅ Proof of deposits and withdrawals (bank records, USDT transactions)
- ✅ Screenshots of your personal cabinet
- ✅ KYC documents you sent to the broker

### Financial Hygiene
- Do not mix personal and trading money
- Use **a separate card/wallet for trading only**
- Track deposits and withdrawals separately from P&L

## 12. A 6–12 Month Roadmap

### Month 1: Theory and Setup
- ☐ Read this guide in full, twice
- ☐ Complete the free [BabyPips School of Pipsology](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex) course
- ☐ Sign up with a regulated broker → **demo only**
- ☐ Install MT5 / TradingView
- ☐ Learn the interface: open/close a trade, place SL/TP, change timeframe
- ☐ Set up a spreadsheet for your trading journal (Google Sheets)

### Month 2: First Strategy and Testing
- ☐ Choose one simple strategy (for example, from section 9)
- ☐ Write its rules down on paper
- ☐ Trade only that strategy on demo, at least 30 trades
- ☐ Log every trade in your journal

### Month 3: Analysis and Refinement
- ☐ Calculate win rate, average R:R, total P&L for the month
- ☐ If P&L < 0 — don't switch to live, refine your approach
- ☐ If P&L > 0 — continue one more month on demo to confirm
- ☐ Read a book by Alexander Elder

### Month 4: Confirming the Result
- ☐ Another 30+ trades on demo using the same strategy
- ☐ Consistent profit? — preparing for live
- ☐ Loss or inconsistent? — back to refinement

### Month 5: Going Live With a Micro-Deposit
- ☐ Deposit **$100–$300** (no more)
- ☐ Micro-lots (0.01) or even a cent account (where 0.01 lot = $10 real, not $1,000)
- ☐ 0.5% risk per trade
- ☐ Same strategy, same journal
- ☐ Be ready to lose that $100–$300

### Month 6+: Growth
- ☐ Only after **3+ months of profit on a live account** — increase your deposit
- ☐ Don't raise your % risk, raise only your deposit
- ☐ Study new elements: fundamentals, additional timeframes, a portfolio approach
- ☐ Never trade money you need to live on

## 14. Final Warning

If you've read this far, you are already ahead of 90% of beginners who immediately rush to open a live account. But remember:

> **Forex does not make people rich quickly. Forex makes disciplined people slightly richer slowly, and undisciplined people much poorer quickly.**

### Six Rules That Will Save Your Deposit

1. **Never trade money you need.** Only money you can afford to lose entirely.
2. **At least 3 months on demo.** No exceptions.
3. **Always place a stop-loss.** The moment you open the trade.
4. **Risk ≤ 1% per trade.** Better 0.5%.
5. **Keep a trading journal.** Every trade, without exception.
