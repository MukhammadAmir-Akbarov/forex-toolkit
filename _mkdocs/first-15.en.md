# First 15 minutes

A short route that prevents a new user from getting lost in the full handbook.
The goal is not to "learn trading in 15 minutes". It is to understand the
workflow and save one safe plan **without opening a live trade**.

<div id="first-15-widget" class="first-15-widget"></div>

!!! warning "Learning and demo only"
    The first plan is for practising the process. Do not use real money until
    demo statistics and journal discipline support the decision.

After these four steps, continue with the full [learning roadmap](roadmap.md).
