# Pre-trade Desk

One sequential screen to size a position, complete the pre-trade checklist and
save a journal-ready plan. Data stays in your browser.

## Strategy lab

Freeze entry conditions, invalidation and maximum risk before a trade sample.
When the rules change, the lab creates a new version so old and new results are
not mixed.

!!! tip "After saving"
    Select **Open plan in journal**. Move the trade from **Plan** to **Open**,
    then add the result, commission and review after closing it. The original
    entry reason stays unchanged. CSV remains available as a fallback export.
