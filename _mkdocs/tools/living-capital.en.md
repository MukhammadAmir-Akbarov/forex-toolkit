# 💼 What it takes to live off trading

!!! abstract "Why this calculation"
    "Quit your job and live off trading" is the promise that sells courses,
    signals and "mentorship". Arguing with it in words is pointless. It is
    easier to do the arithmetic.

    To **withdraw** your monthly living costs, the capital has to be roughly
    **seventy times** larger than they are. That is not pessimism — it is your
    expenses divided by a realistic monthly return.

!!! warning "Educational material — not financial advice"
    This shows an order of magnitude at the return you entered yourself.
    Steady returns do not exist.

## Where the number comes from

The formula is short:

```
earn before tax = expenses ÷ (1 − tax rate)
capital needed  = earn before tax ÷ monthly return
```

Example: you need **$500** a month in hand, at a **1.5%** monthly return, 12%
income tax.

- you must earn `500 ÷ 0.88 = $568` a month;
- capital: `568 ÷ 0.015 = $37,900`.

Plus a buffer: if you withdraw all the profit every month, the very first
losing month has to come out of the capital itself, and it starts shrinking.
So a few months of expenses are held **away** from the trading account.

## Why the return sits in the denominator

This is the main thing the calculator shows. The return does not act linearly:

| Monthly return | Capital for $500 in hand |
|---|---|
| 3% (optimistic) | ≈ $18,900 |
| 1.5% (realistic) | ≈ $37,900 |
| 1% (boring but honest) | ≈ $56,800 |

The gap between "3% a month" and "1% a month" sounds small. In capital it is
**three times**.

The same arithmetic shows why "30% a month" is meaningless: at that return
$1,900 would be enough — and then it is unclear why that person needs your
money.

## What to do with the number

1. **Do not quit your income.** Until the capital is there, trading is not a
   replacement for a salary but a skill you learn alongside one.
2. **Count the time honestly.** The calculator shows how many months the goal
   takes at your savings rate. It is usually years, and that is normal.
3. **Learn not to lose first.** Capital without a working strategy simply
   shrinks faster. Check yourself with the [readiness test](risk-profile.md)
   and the [journal](../journal/web-journal.md).
