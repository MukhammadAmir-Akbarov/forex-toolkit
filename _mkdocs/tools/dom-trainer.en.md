# 🧱 Order Book & Tape Trainer

!!! abstract "What this is"
    The only way to learn order flow is **practice**, not one more article.
    This is a book (DOM) and tape simulator: you watch 25 ticks, then the
    tape stops — and you predict where the next 5 ticks will go. The book
    imbalance here carries a **weak but real** signal — just like a live market.

## How to play

1. Press **“Start round”** — simply watch 25 ticks: which side of the book
   holds more size, what prints hit the tape, whether any are large.
2. The tape stops. Choose **⬆ Up** or **⬇ Down** for the next 5 ticks.
3. Read the breakdown: right or wrong, and how the imbalance related to the outcome.
4. A series is 10 rounds. Your best score is saved in the browser.

## What counts as skill

**7+ out of 10** — you read imbalance better than a coin. **5–6 out of 10** —
coin-flip level: don't be discouraged, that's the trainer's main lesson.
The book gives a **probability, not an answer**, and the signal is weak by
design — on a real market, spread and noise eat even more of it.

Anyone promising you can “see where price will go” from the book is selling
an illusion. The real skill is knowing when the odds are slightly better than
usual — and trading only with an edge from risk management.

## What to carry over to a live market

- **Imbalance beats the last print.** One large print is noise; a persistent
  size advantage on one side is information.
- **A large print against the imbalance is often a trap.** Absorption of a
  big order without price movement says more than the order itself.
- **A prediction is a bet on probability.** Even a correct read loses 3–4
  times out of 10. That's why no skill replaces a stop loss.
