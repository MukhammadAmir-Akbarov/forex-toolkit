# Trading vs a 20% Bank Deposit: Honest Maths

> ⚠️ Educational material, not financial advice. Verified: **2026-06-18**.
> Deposit rates change — check [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz) and your own bank
> before making any decisions.

In Uzbekistan a soum bank deposit yields roughly **18–24% per year with
near-zero risk**. Against that backdrop, an "Instagram trader" promises
"10% per month". Let's work out what that actually means — no emotions,
just the calculators on this site.

!!! tip "The main idea in 10 seconds"
    You already have a **free, risk-free benchmark** — a bank deposit.
    Any "strategy" or "fund manager" must **convincingly beat it on a
    risk-adjusted basis**, otherwise there is no point in risking your money.
    And "return above a deposit AND no risk" does not exist — that is always
    a scheme.

## What a bank deposit gives you

- The Central Bank rate is high, so **soum deposits yield ~18–24% per year**
  (depends on the bank and term — check current figures at your bank).
- Risk is close to zero: individual deposits in Uzbekistan are insured.
- Example: **10 million soum at 20%** = +2 million soum after one year,
  **guaranteed**, while you do nothing.

That is your benchmark. Remember the figure "20% per year" — everything
below is compared against it.

## What "traders" promise — and why it is a pyramid

"10% per month" sounds only slightly above a deposit. But the key word is
**per month**. Apply compound interest:

```
(1 + 0.10)¹² − 1  =  3.14 − 1  =  +213% per year
```

| Promised "10%/month" | That in annual terms | What 10 M soum would become |
|---|---|---|
| after 1 year | +213% | ~31 M soum |
| after 2 years | +885% | ~98 M soum |
| after 3 years | +2986% | ~309 M soum |

**Nobody** in the world achieves this on a stable, ongoing basis. The best
hedge funds return 20–30% **per year**. Warren Buffett — a legend of
investing — averaged ~20% per year **over decades**. And a "mentor" on
Telegram promises the same thing **every month**?

!!! danger "Sign of a pyramid scheme"
    A stable return that is far above a deposit and presented as
    "guaranteed" is almost always a **pyramid**: early "investors" are paid
    with money from new ones until the inflow dries up and the scheme
    collapses. Learn more — [Scam Protection](scam-protection.md).

👉 **Check it yourself:** open the [compound-interest calculator](../tools/compound-calculator.md)
and enter `10%` per month — it will show "not realistic" and the annual equivalent.

## What realistic trading actually delivers

- An experienced, disciplined trader: a rough target of **20–40% per year** —
  and even that comes with drawdowns, bad months, and zero guarantee.
- A beginner in year one: **more often in the red**. According to ESMA data,
  **74–89% of retail traders lose money**.
- Even a good strategy with a 50% win rate and 1:1 risk/reward ratio produces
  **zero** before spreads and commissions — verify it in the
  [Win Rate × R:R calculator](../tools/winrate-rr-calculator.md).

In other words, trading is a **risk taken for a chance** to beat a deposit,
not a "way to earn more than the bank with a guarantee".

## Comparison in one table

| | 🏦 Soum deposit | 📈 Realistic trading | 🚩 "Trader" promises |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Return** | ~18–24% / year | from a loss to +20–40% / year | "10% / month" = +213% / year |
| **Risk** | almost none | high, with drawdowns | loss of up to 100% |
| **Guarantee** | yes (insurance) | no | "yes" — that is a lie |
| **Effort** | 5 minutes to open | years of learning and practice | "do nothing" |
| **Who pays the return** | bank from its margin | the market (if you are right) | new victims (pyramid) |

## When trading still makes sense

Trading is **not a replacement for a deposit** — it is a separate, risky
experiment. A sensible order of priorities:

1. First — an **emergency fund** and paying off expensive debts.
2. Part of your savings — into a **deposit** (that risk-free ~20%).
3. Only **money you can genuinely afford to lose** — into learning to trade:
   demo first, then micro-risk (≤1% of deposit per trade).

If you treat the market as "earn fast instead of a deposit" you will almost
certainly lose. If you treat it as "a skill I develop over years with money
whose loss would not ruin my life" — that is an honest path.

## A 5-second self-check

- Someone promises a return **above a deposit** and also **"no risk"**? →
  That is **100% a scam**. The market physically cannot guarantee income.
- They ask you to hand money to a "manager" or into a "fund" with a fixed
  percentage? → Red flags in [Scam Protection](scam-protection.md).
