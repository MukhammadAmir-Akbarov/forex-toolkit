# 🌙 Swap-Free (Islamic) Accounts

!!! warning "Educational material"
    This is an explanation of mechanics, **not a religious or financial ruling**. For
    questions about the permissibility of forex and the specific conditions of an account,
    consult a qualified specialist and the broker directly.

## What Is a Swap

A **swap** is a fee charged for holding an open position overnight. It arises from the
difference in interest rates between the two currencies in a pair and is applied every
night (often tripled on Wednesdays — to cover the weekend). A swap can be negative
(you pay) or positive (you receive).

For more detail on swap as a trading cost, see the [trading cost calculator](../tools/cost-calculator.md).

## Why This Matters for Muslim Traders

A swap is, by its nature, the **accrual of interest** (riba) for holding a position over
time. For many Muslim traders this makes a standard account unacceptable. Brokers
therefore offer a **swap-free** (Islamic) account where the overnight carry charge is
not applied.

!!! info "Important to understand"
    "Swap-free" removes **only** the overnight swap. Whether margin forex trading is
    permissible at all (leverage, deferred settlement) is a separate question on which
    different opinions exist. This page describes only the technical side.

## How It Works at a Broker

Instead of a swap the broker typically uses one of the following schemes:

| Scheme | What it means |
|---|---|
| **No swap, no replacement** | Overnight carry is free (common on majors). |
| **Fixed admin commission** | Instead of an interest-based swap — a fixed fee per lot after N days of holding. |
| **Widened spread / commission** | Costs are "baked in" to the spread — the total may be more expensive than a standard account. |

**Takeaway:** swap-free ≠ free of charge. Calculate the total cost (spread + commission +
admin fee), not just "no swap". Compare using the [cost calculator](../tools/cost-calculator.md).

## Pitfalls to Watch Out For

- **Not all instruments.** Swap-free status often covers currency pairs and gold, but not
  exotics or certain CFDs.
- **Day limit.** Free overnight carry is frequently capped (e.g. 3–14 nights); beyond that
  an admin commission kicks in.
- **Marketing.** Some brokers use "Islamic account" as a hook while offering worse spreads.
  Always compare the total cost.
- **Carry-trade ban.** The broker may close the account if it detects that swap-free status
  is being exploited purely to avoid negative swaps on long-held positions.

## How to Enable

1. Open a standard account with a regulated broker ([how to choose](brokers-uz.md)).
2. In your personal cabinet find the **Swap-Free / Islamic account** option or contact support.
3. Confirm **in writing**: which instruments are covered, whether there is an admin
   commission and from which day, and whether the spread worsens.
4. Verify on a demo account or with a small volume that the swap is genuinely not charged.

## Red Flags

- A broker promises an "Islamic account" + "guaranteed profit" — that is a [scam](scam-protection.md).
- The broker will not provide written terms for the admin commission.
- The spread on the swap-free account is many times wider than on a standard account — hidden cost.
