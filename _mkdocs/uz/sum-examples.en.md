# 🇺🇿 Examples in Soum and Uzbek Terms

!!! abstract "Why this page exists"
    Most textbooks calculate in dollars, but you deposit and withdraw in **soum (UZS)**.
    Here you will find realistic examples with UZS ⇄ USD conversions and a glossary of
    forex terms in Uzbek, so that vocabulary from English-language videos makes sense.

## How Much Is That in Soum

Exchange rates change — these examples use **1 USD ≈ 12,500 soum** (check the current
rate at [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz)). All in-browser calculators work in USD; convert at
the current rate.

| Deposit | In dollars | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 250 000 сум | ~$100 | Minimum for **demo-psychology** on a real account. Do not expect profit. |
| 2 500 000 сум | ~$200 | A realistic "learning" start. 1% risk = $2 per trade. |
| 6 250 000 сум | ~$500 | More noticeable results, but this is **not** "quit your job" money. |
| 12 500 000 сум | ~$1 000 | A serious deposit for a beginner. Do not bring your last savings. |

!!! danger "The golden rule"
    Only bring money you can lose without going into debt or suffering serious stress.
    Forex is not a way to "quickly double" your savings. Most beginners lose their first
    deposit — that is part of the learning process, which is exactly why it should be small.

## Example: Risk in Soum

Deposit **2 500 000 soum** (~$200), risk **1%** per trade:

```
Risk per trade = 200 × 1% = $2.00 ≈ 25 000 soum
```

Twenty-five thousand soum per trade sounds small, but that is precisely how a deposit
survives. Calculate the position size for this risk in the
[position calculator](../tools/position-calculator.md).

## Example: Costs in Soum

40 small trades per month, 1-pip spread, 0.1 lot, EUR/USD:

```
Costs ≈ $40/month ≈ 500 000 soum per month ≈ 6 000 000 soum per year
```

On a 2,500,000-soum deposit, annual overtrading costs can **exceed the deposit itself**.
Calculate your own costs in the [cost calculator](../tools/cost-calculator.md).

## Tax in Soum

Net annual profit is subject to personal income tax at 12% (for residents). Estimate
the amount due and the declaration deadline with the [tax calculator](tax-calculator.md).
Verify the current rate and procedure at [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz) — this is an
educational estimate, not tax advice.
