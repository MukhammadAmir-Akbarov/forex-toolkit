# Communities and Resources for Traders from CIS / Uzbekistan

> ⚠️ This list is general — specific groups and channels are constantly created and closed. Use it as a search guide. **Do not subscribe to paid "signals" — it is almost always a loss of money.**

## Where to Find Russian-Speaking Communities

### Telegram

**Where to search (categories, not names):**
- Search in Telegram for: "forex education", "technical analysis", "trading journal"
- **Do NOT subscribe** to channels offering "signals" or "guaranteed profit"
- Good channels are educational, review trades **after the fact**, and never say "enter right now"

**What a legitimate channel should have:**
- ✅ Educational posts
- ✅ The author's OWN trade reviews (including losses)
- ✅ Open track record / statistics
- ✅ Free access to core content

**Red flags:**
- 🚩 Paid subscription with profit promises
- 🚩 "VIP channel with exclusive signals"
- 🚩 Only winning trades ever shown
- 🚩 Pressure to deposit large sums with "their" broker (affiliate scheme)

## YouTube — Education (with a filter)

**What to look for:**
- Channels where the author trades on a demo **openly** and shows a real account
- Long videos of 30+ minutes with strategy breakdowns
- "From scratch" educational playlists

**What to avoid:**
- 🚩 "From zero to a million in 30 days"
- 🚩 "Secret strategy with 95% win rate"
- 🚩 "Quit my job — now earning $10k/month"
- 🚩 Very short clips on "how to make money right now"

**Well-known international channels (for intermediate level):**
- The Trading Channel (EN)
- ForexSignals TV (EN)
- Rayner Teo (EN, very systematic)

## Local Uzbekistan Resources

> The trading scene in Uzbekistan is young. Many Uzbek channels are local brokers doing marketing, not real education.

**What is worth looking for:**
- Uzbek-language trading groups on Facebook
- Local educational centers (with caution — most are paid courses of low quality)
- Local crypto and FX community meetups in Tashkent

**What to definitely avoid:**
- Any "investment clubs" with a mandatory deposit
- Paid courses with a "guaranteed job at a prop firm"
- "Trade on your behalf" services

## Paid Resources — When It Makes Sense

Paying for education **makes sense** if:

- ✅ After 6+ months of independent trading
- ✅ You have specific questions not covered by free sources
- ✅ The course author is a practitioner, not an "info-guru"
- ✅ The price is reasonable ($100–500, not $5000+)
- ✅ Specific skills: e.g. a concrete strategy with a detailed breakdown

**Do NOT pay if:**
- 🚩 You are just starting out (free materials are sufficient)
- 🚩 The course promises "guaranteed profit"
- 🚩 "Closed chat" for $200/month
- 🚩 "Signals"

## Final Recommendation on Communities

1. **80% of the time — on your own** (reading, backtesting, demo)
2. **15% — neutral sources** (BabyPips, Forex Factory, Reddit)
3. **5% — communicating with traders** (forums, not paid groups)

**0% — "gurus", "signals", "closed chats".**
