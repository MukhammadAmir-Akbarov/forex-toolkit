# ⚖️ Legal FAQ for Traders from Uzbekistan

!!! warning "This is not legal advice"
    This section answers common questions in plain language and is **educational**
    in nature. Laws and interpretations change. Before making real decisions,
    consult official sources ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz), [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz),
    [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz)) or a qualified lawyer.

## Is it legal to trade forex in Uzbekistan?

Private individuals are **not prohibited** from trading their own funds through a
foreign broker. Uzbekistan has **no dedicated forex regulator**, and local banks do
not offer such trading directly — which is why foreign brokers are used (see
[how to choose a broker](brokers-uz.md)). The main risk is not "legality" but the
**absence of local protection**: if a foreign broker defrauds you, there is almost
nowhere to turn.

## Do I need to register as a sole trader / business?

For trading **your own** money as an individual — generally no. Registration and a
different tax regime become relevant when the activity becomes **entrepreneurial**
(for example, managing other people's funds, attracting investors). That is a
different status — consult a tax adviser to clarify.

!!! danger "Managing other people's money"
    Taking other people's money "into management" with a promise of profit and
    without a licence is a path to criminal and civil problems, and is a classic
    hallmark of a [financial pyramid scheme](scam-protection.md).
    Do not do this, and do not hand your money to such "managers".

## Do I need to pay tax on profits?

Yes. A resident's net annual profit is subject to **personal income tax (NDFL) 12%**.
The **annual result** is declared (profits minus losses), not each individual trade.
Estimate the amount and deadline with the [tax calculator](tax-calculator.md);
the procedure is on [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz).

- Keep your **broker statements** for at least 3 years.
- If you made a loss for the year, no tax arises, but keep the statement anyway.
- Verify the exact declaration procedure with the tax authority — wording changes.

## Can I deposit and withdraw funds?

Yes, but only through **legal channels** and in compliance with currency regulation.
How to do this safely (UZCARD/HUMO cards, international cards, legal methods) and
which schemes are **dangerous** — covered in detail in the
[money withdrawal guide](withdrawal-guide.md).

!!! danger "Illegal P2P exchangers"
    Transferring funds through "private individuals" and grey P2P schemes may
    violate currency legislation and is frequently used by scammers. Use only the
    official methods provided by your broker and bank.

## What you absolutely should NOT do

- ❌ Take other people's money "into management" without a licence.
- ❌ Recruit people with promises of guaranteed profit (pyramid scheme).
- ❌ Use grey P2P exchangers for deposits or withdrawals.
- ❌ Conceal regular income from the tax authority.
- ❌ Trust "signal" and "copy-trading" channels that promise a fixed percentage return.
