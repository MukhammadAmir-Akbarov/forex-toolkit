# Law and Forex in Uzbekistan: FAQ for Beginners

> ⚠️ Educational material, **not legal or tax advice**.
> Verified: **2026-06-18**. Laws and regulations change — before taking any
> action check primary sources: [lex.uz](https://lex.uz), [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz),
> [napp.uz](https://napp.uz), [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz).

!!! abstract "Short version (if you are in a hurry)"
    - **Forex.** Trading through a foreign regulated broker as an individual is
      not directly prohibited, but **there is no separate Uzbek forex regulator
      and no local retail-forex licences** — this is a gray area, check for
      yourself.
    - **Crypto.** Strictly regulated: only through licensed NAPP providers,
      **P2P is illegal for residents**.
    - **Tax.** Income must be declared: personal income tax **12%**, filing
      deadline **April 1**.
    - **Main red flag.** "Trust management" with a guaranteed return from a
      private individual is almost always an **illegal scheme**.

## Who regulates forex and crypto in Uzbekistan?

- **Crypto assets** are regulated by **NAPP** (National Agency for Advanced
  Projects). Only operations through providers listed in its registry are legal;
  everything else (including P2P) is outside the law.
- **Forex.** As of the verification date, **no dedicated state regulator
  specifically for retail forex and no forex-dealer licensing regime in
  Uzbekistan could be found**. Currency flows and transfers are regulated by
  currency legislation and the Central Bank ([cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz)). That is
  why most people trade through **foreign** regulated brokers.

!!! info "Honest note on the gray area"
    If someone claims to hold "an Uzbekistan forex licence" — ask for the
    number and verify it with the regulator they name. We did not find a
    separate forex-dealer registry in Uzbekistan; do not take such claims at
    face value.

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [napp.uz](https://napp.uz), [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz), [lex.uz](https://lex.uz). Confidence: crypto — high; forex oversight — noted as uncertainty._

## Is it legal for me, as a resident, to trade forex?

The act of trading currencies through a foreign broker as an individual is **not
criminalised**. But two things matter:

1. **Legal deposit/withdrawal of funds.** Use banking channels (SWIFT,
   Visa/Mastercard, Wise) — they are legal and easier for record-keeping. Full
   details — [Money Withdrawal Guide](withdrawal-guide.md).
2. **Declaring income** (see the tax section below).

What you **cannot** do:

- hand money to a "manager" or a "fund" in exchange for a fixed percentage;
- exchange crypto outside licensed providers;
- work with a broker that lacks a serious regulator. Choose a broker licensed by
  **FCA / CySEC / ASIC** and verify the number **on the regulator's own website**
  — see [Brokers for Uzbekistan](brokers-uz.md).

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz), [napp.uz](https://napp.uz)._

## What about crypto (USDT etc.)?

- Exchanging USDT for soums is **legal only through licensed NAPP providers**:
  CoinPay ([coinpay.uz](https://coinpay.uz)), Telegram Wallet, exchanges Kobea,
  Coinpay, Asterium. Current registry — [napp.uz](https://napp.uz).
- **P2P** (Binance and other platforms) for Uzbekistan residents has been
  **closed since 26 May 2025 and is outside the law** — according to NAPP
  clarifications, citizens' operations through P2P are illegal and carry
  liability (including criminal liability for illegal circulation of crypto
  assets).
- Income received **through licensed providers** is, according to NAPP
  clarifications, exempt from tax.

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [napp.uz](https://napp.uz), [spot.uz](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/05/28/blocked-p2p/), [gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/en/2025/01/17/binance/). Confidence: high._

## Do I need to pay tax on trading income?

Yes. In brief:

- Rate — **personal income tax 12%** (current for 2026).
- You declare the **net annual result** (profits − losses), **not each trade**
  individually.
- Filing deadline — **by April 1** of the following year, via the personal
  account at [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz). Tax authority contact centre — **1198**.
- Keep broker statements and transfer confirmations for **at least 3 years**.

🧮 Calculate your tax amount: [Tax Calculator](tax-calculator.md).

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz), [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz). Always verify the rate and procedure at soliq.uz._

## How to deposit and withdraw money legally?

- **Banking channels** — SWIFT, Visa/Mastercard, Wise — are legal and
  convenient for tax reporting.
- **Crypto** — only through licensed providers (see above).
- Do not split transfers to "hide" them, and do not withdraw to someone else's
  wallet — this creates problems rather than solving them. Full breakdown of
  channels and fees — [Money Withdrawal Guide](withdrawal-guide.md).

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz), [napp.uz](https://napp.uz)._

## Is "trust management", "investment funds", "PAMM" from private individuals legal?

Collecting money from the public with a promise of income is a **licensed
activity**. A private individual from Instagram or Telegram who collects money
to a personal **Humo/Uzcard** card under "a fixed percentage" or "trust
management" is almost always an **illegal pyramid scheme or fraud**.

!!! danger "The rule that protects against 90% of schemes"
    **Nobody can guarantee income in the market**. Any "guaranteed profit" =
    fraud by definition. Red flags and a verification checklist —
    [Scam Protection](scam-protection.md).

If you have already transferred money to a fraudster: gather evidence and file a
report with law enforcement (service **102**) and/or the prosecutor's office —
the sooner the better.

_Verified 2026-06-18 · sources: [napp.uz](https://napp.uz), service 102._
