# What is Forex? In plain words for beginners in Uzbekistan

> ⚠️ Educational material, not financial advice. Last verified: **2026-06-18**.
> This page answers the very first questions a beginner asks ("Forex nima?",
> "is forex halal?", "is it legal?") honestly — without promises of easy money.

If you are searching for "Forex nima?" or "what is forex" — the results are
usually full of broker ads and "mentors". Here there are no ads: what it
actually is, whether you can make money from it, and how to start **the
right way**.

## Forex — what it is in plain words

**Forex (Forex, FX)** is the global currency exchange market. When you exchange
dollars for soums at a bank — you have already performed a currency transaction.
On the forex market the same thing happens at a massive scale between banks,
funds and traders all over the world, and currency prices change constantly.

A trader tries to **profit from the change in exchange rates**: for example,
buy euros for dollars at a lower rate and sell at a higher rate. The difference
is the profit — or a loss if the rate moved against you.

!!! info "The most important thing to understand from the start"
    Forex is **not "work on the internet" and not "passive income"**. It is a
    market with high risk where, according to ESMA data, **74–89% of beginners
    lose money**. It is possible to earn, but it is a skill that takes **years**
    to develop — not a week following "signals from Telegram".

## Is forex halal? (Is it religiously permissible?)

This is a common and important question. The honest answer: **a religious
verdict is given not by a website and not by a broker**, but by a qualified
person in that field — someone you should consult directly.

From a technical standpoint we can only explain the **mechanics**: on a
standard account, rolling a position overnight incurs a **swap** (interest,
riba). For those who avoid interest, brokers offer **swap-free (Islamic)
accounts** — but these have their own nuances around fees. A full breakdown
is on the [Swap-free (Islamic) accounts](swap-free.md) page.

## Is it legal to trade forex in Uzbekistan?

In short: trading through a **foreign regulated broker** as an individual is
not directly prohibited, but there is no separate Uzbek forex regulator, and
**crypto** is strictly regulated (P2P is outside the law). Income must be
**declared** (personal income tax 12%).

Full breakdown with sources — [Law and Forex in Uzbekistan](law-faq.md).

## Can you actually make money?

You can — but let us set honest expectations:

- This is **risk**, not guaranteed income. Any "profit guarantee" is a lie.
- A realistic benchmark even for professionals is tens of percent **per year**
  (with drawdowns), not "10% per month". Why "10%/month" = a pyramid scheme is
  shown on the [Trading vs 20% deposit](trading-vs-deposit.md) page.
- In Uzbekistan a **risk-free bank deposit** yields ~18–24% per year. Any
  strategy must **beat this accounting for risk** — otherwise there is no
  point in taking the risk.

## How to start correctly (and not blow your deposit)

1. **Theory first.** Go through the basics — what is a pip, a lot, leverage,
   a stop-loss. Start with the [main guide](../forex-guide.md) and the
   [learning roadmap](../roadmap.md).
2. **Demo account.** Trade with virtual money until your strategy becomes
   consistent. Real money comes later.
3. **Risk management from day one.** No more than ~1% of your deposit per
   trade. Calculate your position size in the
   [calculator](../tools/position-calculator.md).
4. **Only spare money.** Not a loan, not your last savings, not "housing money".

## Watch out: scammers profit from beginners

The flow of beginners into forex in Uzbekistan is large, and scammers exploit
this. Before paying anyone — read [Scam Protection](scam-protection.md):

- "Guaranteed income", "trust management", transfer to a personal card —
  red flags.
- A "mentor" with screenshots of profits and a Lamborghini — almost always
  an affiliate deal or an outright scam.
