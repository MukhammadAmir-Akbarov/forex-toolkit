# Scam Protection: How Not to Lose Money on "Trading" in Uzbekistan

!!! tip "Check the broker before you deposit"
    The [broker check](../tools/broker-check.md) builds direct links to the FCA,
    CySEC, ASIC, NFA and FINMA registries and helps you tick off red flags.


> ⚠️ Educational material, not financial or legal advice.
> Last verified: **2026-06-11**. Legal status and contacts change — check
> primary sources ([napp.uz](https://napp.uz)) before taking any action.

!!! danger "One rule that protects against 90% of schemes"
    **Nobody can guarantee income in the market.** If someone promises you
    "10% per month", "risk-free income", "we'll double your deposit" — this is **not
    trading, it is a money-extraction scheme**. The real market involves risk: according
    to ESMA data, 74–89% of retail traders lose money. A guaranteed return = a lie
    by definition.

In Uzbekistan the flow of beginners into forex is growing faster than financial
literacy — and scammers exploit this. Below is how to spot a scam **before**
you send any money.

## 🚩 7 Red Flags

1. **Guaranteed returns.** "From 5% per week", "fixed profit".
   There are no guarantees on the real market — only probabilities.
2. **"Trust management".** A stranger offers to trade your money "for a
   percentage". In 99% of cases the money simply disappears. Handing capital
   to an unlicensed individual for management is almost always a scam.
3. **Money to a personal card.** They ask you to transfer to a **personal
   Humo/Uzcard account**, USDT to a personal wallet, "top up via my assistant".
   A licensed provider does not work this way.
4. **Time pressure.** "Spots are running out", "the offer is only for today",
   "you have to act before the market closes". Urgency disables critical
   thinking — that is exactly what the scammer needs.
5. **"Mentor" on Instagram/Telegram** with screenshots of Lamborghinis, stacks
   of cash, and "student reports". Profit screenshots can be faked in a minute.
6. **Impossible to withdraw money.** Deposits are accepted instantly, but for
   withdrawals — "pay the tax/fee/verification first". This is the **second
   turn of the scam**: you should never pay to withdraw your own money.
7. **Referral pyramid.** The main income is "bring friends", not trading.
   This is an MLM pyramid dressed up as trading.

## ✅ How to Verify Before Paying

!!! info "5-Minute Checklist"
    - [ ] **Licence in Uzbekistan.** Any work with crypto assets is legal
          only through providers listed in the **NAPP registry** — [napp.uz](https://napp.uz).
          Not in the registry → do not engage.
    - [ ] **Broker's regulator.** A serious forex broker is regulated (FCA,
          CySEC, ASIC, etc.). Verify the licence number on the **regulator's own
          website**, not on the broker's website.
    - [ ] **Where the money goes.** Only official company payment details, not
          a personal card. A transfer to an individual = red flag.
    - [ ] **Demo first.** A legitimate platform provides a demo account. "Real
          account only, no demo" — reason to be cautious.
    - [ ] **Search for reviews.** Company name + words "scam", "won't withdraw",
          "fraud" — in English, Russian, and Uzbek. Recent complaints = stop.

## 🎭 Typical Schemes (by type, not by name)

- **Signal groups with a "guarantee".** You pay for "signals" that drain your
  deposit; the seller earns from subscriptions, not from the market.
- **Copy-trading with a profit promise.** Copying trades is a legitimate
  tool, but a **guaranteed return** on top of it turns it into a scam.
- **"Training" with a mandatory deposit** at a "partner" broker —
  the mentor earns a commission on your losses (CPA affiliate deal).
- **Fake exchanges and apps.** A polished interface, fabricated charts,
  funds cannot be withdrawn. They often disappear within a couple of months.
- **Scam "prop firms".** They charge a fee for a "challenge" that is technically
  impossible to pass, and they do not pay out under the rules.

## 🆘 If You Have Already Sent Money

!!! warning "Act calmly and quickly"
    1. **Stop all payments.** No "top-ups to unlock withdrawal" — that is a second scam.
    2. **Block your card / stop transfers** via your bank or app if you suspect
       further charges.
    3. **Collect evidence:** correspondence, receipts, links, names, card numbers,
       screenshots of the website (it may disappear).
    4. **File a report** with law enforcement (service **102**) and/or the
       prosecutor's office. The sooner you act, the better the chances.
    5. **Warn others** — post in the [project Discussions](https://github.com/MukhammadAmir-Akbarov/forex-toolkit/discussions)
       so that beginners can see the scheme.

    Important: recovering money is not always possible. That is why the main
    protection is **not transferring** money when the red flags above appear.
