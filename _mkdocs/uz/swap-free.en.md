# Swap-Free (Islamic) Accounts: How Not to Overpay on a "Halal Account"

> ⚠️ Educational material about **cost mechanics**, not financial and **not
> religious** advice. Last verified: **2026-06-18**. Whether trading is
> permissible from a religious standpoint is outside the scope of a calculator —
> consult a knowledgeable person on that question. This page is only about
> money and fees.

Many brokers offer **swap-free** (also called "Islamic" or "halal") accounts —
with no swap charged for holding a position overnight. This sounds like "trading
without extra fees", and for traders who avoid interest (riba) it is an important
option. But "swap-free" **does not mean "free"**. Let's break down where the
costs are hidden.

## What a Swap Is (and Why It Exists)

A **swap** is a charge (or a credit) for keeping a position open **overnight**.
In essence it is the difference between the interest rates of the two currencies
in a pair. A swap can be negative (you pay) or positive (you receive) — but for
a beginner it is most often a **cost** that quietly accumulates with every night.

👉 To see how much swap and other costs eat up over a month or year, use the
[trade cost calculator](../tools/cost-calculator.md).

## What a Swap-Free Account Does

On an Islamic account the broker **does not charge a swap** — neither negative
nor positive. This is designed for traders who, for religious reasons, do not
want to deal with interest.

!!! warning "The main trap: swap is replaced by a different fee"
    A broker is a business and it **does not work at a loss**. If it removes
    the swap, it almost always **compensates in another way**. Typical forms:

    - **Fixed holding fee** — for example, "free for the first 3–5 nights,
      then a fixed charge per lot per night". On long positions this can end
      up **more expensive** than the regular swap.
    - **Wider spread** — the gap between the buy and sell price is slightly
      larger, and you pay it on **every** trade, even intraday ones.
    - **One-time fee for "Islamic" account status** or an elevated per-lot
      commission.

    Bottom line: "swap-free" is a **redistribution** of costs, not their
    disappearance.

## How to Check Whether an Account Is Actually Beneficial

!!! info "Checklist before opening"
    - [ ] **Read the swap-free terms** on the broker's website: from which day
          and exactly what fee begins ("administration fee", "holding fee").
    - [ ] **Compare the spread** of the standard and Islamic account for your
          trading pairs.
    - [ ] **Calculate the full cost** of your typical scenario (trade frequency,
          average holding period) in the
          [trade cost calculator](../tools/cost-calculator.md):
          plug in both "swap = 0" and "swap = 0 but spread/commission higher" —
          you'll see the real difference.
    - [ ] **Check for a "day cap"**: some brokers grant swap-free only for N
          days, then retroactively apply a commission.
    - [ ] **Broker's regulator** — separate from account type. Start with a
          reliable broker (FCA/CySEC/ASIC), then choose an Islamic account
          there. See [Brokers for Uzbekistan](brokers-uz.md).

## When Swap-Free Makes Sense

- It is **fundamentally important** to you to avoid interest (riba) — then this
  is the only suitable option, and the only question is choosing an account with
  the **minimum compensating fee**.
- You **hold positions for a long time** (swing/position trading) and the regular
  negative swap on your pairs is large — sometimes the fixed fee works out cheaper.

But if you trade **intraday** and close everything before midnight server time,
swap does not affect you at all — and an "Islamic" account with a wider spread
will only cost you **more**.

## Red Flags

- "Swap-free **and** no other fees whatsoever, and the spread is the same as a
  standard account" — this does not exist. The cost is hidden somewhere; find it
  before you open the account.
- A "halal account" used as a **marketing lure** by an unregulated broker or a
  "mentor" driving you through an affiliate link. Account type does not make a
  scam legal — check [Scam Protection](scam-protection.md).
- A promise that an Islamic account "guarantees profit" or is "risk-free". Account
  type **does not change** the fact that the market involves risk.
