## Regulation

Uzbekistan has **no dedicated forex regulator**. Local banks do not provide forex trading directly. Most Uzbek traders use **foreign brokers** regulated by well-known authorities:

| Regulator | Country | Trust level |
|---|---|---|
| **FCA** | United Kingdom | 🟢 Very high |
| **CySEC** | Cyprus / EU | 🟢 High (EU standard) |
| **ASIC** | Australia | 🟢 High |
| **FSCA** | South Africa | 🟡 Medium |
| **CBCS** | Curaçao | 🔴 Low (fast registration) |
| **SVG FSA** | Saint Vincent | ⛔ Not considered real regulation |

**Rule:** the stricter the regulator, the harder it is to "get your money back" from them, but the lower the chance of falling victim to a scam.

## Specifics for UZ

### Deposit / Withdrawal Methods

See [Withdrawal Guide](withdrawal-guide.md) (detailed).

In brief:
- **Visa/Mastercard**: works both ways, fee 1–3%
- **USDT (Tether)**: cheapest and fastest — via Binance / Bybit
- **Wise / Payeer**: intermediary, limits apply
- **SWIFT transfer**: only for large amounts ($5,000+)

### Taxation

In Uzbekistan, forex profits are formally subject to **12% personal income tax**. In practice, the tax authority rarely checks, but **it is your obligation** to declare. See `uz/tax-calculator.py` for the calculation.

### Country Restrictions

A good broker **should not restrict** UZ residents. If registration requires a VPN or proxy — that is a bad sign.

## Broker Selection Checklist

- [ ] Regulation: FCA, ASIC, or CySEC (minimum)
- [ ] EUR/USD spread on an ECN account: ≤ 1 pip (commission-free: ≤ 1.5)
- [ ] Minimum deposit: ≤ $100
- [ ] Micro-lots available (0.01)
- [ ] Support in Russian or English (not machine translation)
- [ ] Withdrawal processed within ≤ 5 business days
- [ ] Rating > 3.5 on independent platforms (minimum 100 reviews)
- [ ] "Guaranteed profit" is not mentioned in their marketing
