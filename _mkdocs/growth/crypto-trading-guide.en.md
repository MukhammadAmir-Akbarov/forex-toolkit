# Crypto Trading Guide — A Parallel World

> If you are in Uzbekistan — crypto is more relevant than forex. This guide shows: what carries over from forex, what is different, and where to start.

## ⚠️ Risks First

Crypto is **even riskier** than forex:
- Volatility of 5–15% per day is normal (forex: 1%)
- Many coins lose 90%+ over months
- Regulation is weaker
- Exchanges sometimes **steal deposits** (FTX, Mt.Gox, Quadriga)
- High risk of fraud (rug-pulls, exit scams)

**If you lost money in forex — you will lose it faster in crypto.**

## What Carries Over from Forex

| From forex | In crypto |
|---|---|
| Candles and patterns | Exactly the same |
| EMA, RSI, MACD | Work the same way |
| Support/resistance | Work the same way |
| Stop-loss | **MANDATORY** |
| 1% risk management | **Stricter — 0.5%** (higher volatility) |
| R:R 1:2 | Same logic |
| Journal | Just as critical |
| Psychology | The same |

**Good news:** what you learned in forex is 80% applicable here.

## What Is Different

### 1. The Market Is Open 24/7
- No "weekends"
- No "session closes"
- This will **wear you down psychologically** — there are no natural pauses

### 2. Massive Volatility
- Bitcoin can move 5–10% in a day — that is **normal**
- Altcoins (small coins) — 30–100% in a week
- Stops must be **wider**, position sizes — **smaller**

### 3. No "Fundamental Value"
- Bitcoin pays no dividends
- There is no GDP, inflation, employment data
- Main drivers:
  - Regulation (Trump support / China ban)
  - Bitcoin Halving (every 4 years)
  - Institutional adoption (ETF, BlackRock)
  - Technology updates (Ethereum upgrades)

### 4. Liquidity Concentration
- **80% of volume** is in Bitcoin and Ethereum
- Altcoins can be "pumped" by individual traders
- In news-driven pumps — real breakouts reverse quickly

### 5. Crypto Exchanges vs Forex Brokers

| | Forex broker | Crypto exchange |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation | FCA, CySEC, ASIC | CFTC (USA), MAS (Singapore) |
| Funds custody | In broker account | On exchange (risky!) or cold wallet |
| Leverage | up to 1:30 (EU), 1:500 (offshore) | 1:5 – 1:125 on futures |
| Access from UZ | OK | Most accept |
| Most reliable | IC Markets, Pepperstone | Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase |

## What to Trade as a Crypto Beginner

### ✅ Only **BTC/USDT** and **ETH/USDT**
- Highest liquidity
- Narrowest spreads
- Least manipulation
- Readable charts

### ❌ None of the following:
- Meme coins (DOGE, SHIB, PEPE) — a lottery
- Freshly listed coins
- Micro-caps (< $1B)
- Futures with leverage > 1:10 (for beginners)

## Where to Trade

### Spot trading (recommended for beginners)

You buy a coin for USDT — hold it → sell higher / lower.
- **No leverage** = you won't blow up instantly
- You can **hold long-term** (HODL)
- Suitable for trend trading

**Exchanges:**
- **Binance** — largest, but KYC is required
- **Bybit** — user-friendly interface, popular in CIS
- **OKX** — many instruments, convenient P2P for UZ
- **Coinbase** — USA, easier for beginners, but expensive
- **Kraken** — USA, very secure

### Futures trading (for experienced traders)

Contracts with leverage 1:5 – 1:125. **Very dangerous** for beginners.

**Do not go here without 1+ year of spot experience.**

## How to Start — Step by Step

### 1. Preparation (1 week)
- [ ] Read [forex-guide.md](../forex-guide.md) — the foundation is necessary
- [ ] Read the Bitcoin white paper (https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)
- [ ] Learn the terms: blockchain, mining, halving, gas fees
- [ ] Follow quality sources (not "gurus"):
  - **The Block** (news)
  - **Bankless** (podcast)
  - **CoinGecko** (data)

### 2. Exchange (1 week)
- [ ] Register on one of the reliable exchanges
- [ ] Complete KYC (passport, selfie)
- [ ] Enable 2FA (Google Authenticator or YubiKey)
- [ ] Deposit **$10–50 for testing** via P2P → USDT

### 3. Demo trading (1–2 months)
- [ ] Most exchanges have **paper trading** (Binance Futures Demo, Bybit Testnet)
- [ ] Trade on demo at least **30 trades**
- [ ] Keep a journal

### 4. Small real amounts (3 months)
- [ ] **Spot BTC/USDT** only
- [ ] Position size: **0.5% of deposit** per trade
- [ ] Journal every trade
- [ ] No leverage

### 5. Scaling up (if consistently profitable)
- [ ] Add ETH/USDT
- [ ] Possibly small positions in Top-10 altcoins
- [ ] Very carefully — try futures with 1:3 leverage

## Security

### The Golden Rule: **Not your keys, not your coins**

If your coins are on an **exchange** — they are **not yours** until you withdraw them. An exchange can:
- Freeze your account
- Go bankrupt
- Be hacked
- Face sanctions

### What to Do with Profits

Large amounts → **cold wallet** (offline):
- **Ledger** ($79+)
- **Trezor** ($99+)

Small amounts (< $500) — leave on the exchange for trading.

### Do NOT Do This

- ❌ Storing passwords in the cloud without encryption
- ❌ Screenshots of your seed phrase in your gallery
- ❌ Accessing the exchange over unsecured Wi-Fi
- ❌ Clicking any Telegram links about crypto

## Taxes in UZ for Crypto

As of 2026 in Uzbekistan:
- Income from crypto is subject to declaration
- Rates are the same as for other types of income (~12%)
- ⚠️ Regulation is changing — check the current rules at soliq.uz

## Top-10 Mistakes Crypto Beginners Make

1. ❌ FOMO into a pump (when the coin has already risen 50%)
2. ❌ Buying "next 100x" altcoins from Telegram
3. ❌ Using leverage from day one
4. ❌ Storing everything on an exchange without 2FA
5. ❌ Ignoring stop-losses
6. ❌ Doxing (being public) on Discord/Telegram
7. ❌ Signing unknown contracts in MetaMask
8. ❌ "Staking" in unknown protocols = rug-pull risk
9. ❌ Comparing yourself to others' "success" on social media
10. ❌ Emotional selling during market crashes

## Final Thoughts

Crypto is a **market where money is easy to make and easy to lose**. Most beginners lose.

**The best strategy for a beginner:** Bitcoin DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) — buy a fixed amount **once a week** for 3–5 years, without trying to "catch the bottom". This works better than 99% of active traders.

If you want to **actively trade crypto** — master forex first. Discipline and risk management are the same. Crypto simply has **more volatility**.
