# Stocks Basics — Diversification and Long-Term Investing

> If you want to **build wealth** rather than "trade" — this is **much safer** than forex. A parallel path.

## Forex vs Stocks vs ETF — The Key Difference

| | Forex | Stocks | ETF |
|---|---|---|---|
| What you buy | Right to a price difference | A share in a company | A basket of stocks |
| Dividends | No | Sometimes | Often |
| Horizon | Days–weeks | Months–years | **Years–decades** |
| Leverage | 1:30–500 | 1:5 max | 1:5 max |
| Ruin risk | High | Medium | Low |
| Active management | Constant | Regular | **Minimal** |
| Suitable for beginners | ❌ | ⚠️ Experienced only | ✅ Yes |

## Main Advice: Start with ETFs

**ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)** — a basket of dozens-to-thousands of stocks traded like a single share.

**Why:**
- ✅ Instant diversification (instead of 1 stock — 500)
- ✅ Minimum effort — buy and hold
- ✅ Low fees (0.03–0.5% per year)
- ✅ Historical return of 7–10% per year (S&P 500 over 30 years)
- ✅ Less emotion — no need to "pick stocks"

## Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) Strategy

The core strategy of a passive investor:

**Once a month** you buy VOO for a fixed amount. You **do not try** to "catch the bottom" or "exit at the top".

```
January:   bought VOO for $200 at $570 → 0.35 shares
February:  bought for $200 at $590 → 0.34 shares
March:     bought for $200 at $545 (market fell) → 0.37 shares
April:     bought for $200 at $600 → 0.33 shares
May:       bought for $200 at $620 → 0.32 shares
...

Over 10 years → you have ~40 shares of VOO ≈ $25,000 (at 8%/year growth)
```

**The main rule:**
- ✅ Every month, on the same day
- ✅ The same fixed amount
- ✅ **Never** try to "predict" the market
- ✅ Reinvest dividends

## Long-Term Math

If you start investing $200/month in the S&P 500 at age 25:

| Years | Amount invested | Capital (at 8%/year) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | $12,000 | $14,600 |
| 10 | $24,000 | $36,500 |
| 20 | $48,000 | $117,600 |
| 30 | $72,000 | $283,100 |
| **40** | **$96,000** | **$622,000** |

**$200/month → $622,000 over 40 years.** That is the power of compound interest.

Forex rarely delivers consistent results better than this, especially accounting for losses.

## When to CHOOSE Stocks Over Forex

If you:
- ✅ Want to **simply accumulate** capital
- ✅ Are willing to hold for **10+ years**
- ✅ Do not want to **actively spend time**
- ✅ Want **less emotional stress**
- ✅ Are aged 25–40 (long time horizon)

→ **Stocks/ETF** is for you, not forex.

## When Forex Might Be Better

If you:
- ⚠️ Are willing to **work actively** every day
- ⚠️ Have completed training and are psychologically resilient
- ⚠️ Want **additional** income on top of investing
- ⚠️ Accept high risk

→ Forex as a **supplement**, not a replacement for ETF.

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Short-timeframe stocks trading** (minutes, hours)
- You compete against Wall Street HFT algorithms
- Chances for a beginner are close to zero

❌ **Buying individual stocks** "on intuition"
- 80% of active traders underperform the S&P 500
- Tesla? AMD? — you'll try to "guess" and end up losing

❌ **Options / derivatives**
- Complex and risky
- Most people lose money

❌ **"Penny stocks"** (stocks under $5)
- 90% are scams (pump & dump)
- Low liquidity

## Strategy in One Line

> **"Buy a broad ETF and hold it for 30 years. Don't open your terminal more than once a month."**

This is **boring**, but it **works** for 95% of people. Forex is a **profession**. ETF is a **tool**.

## What Is Better for You

Do not choose **either forex or ETF**. The best strategy for most people:

1. **80% of money** → ETF (VOO + VXUS + BND) — long-term accumulation
2. **15% of money** → Free funds / emergency cushion
3. **5% of money** → Forex / crypto (if you really want to trade actively) — this is **money you can afford to lose**

This way you **guarantee** long-term growth. And you still get the satisfaction of active trading.
