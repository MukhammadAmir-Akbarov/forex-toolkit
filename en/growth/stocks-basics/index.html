    Stocks Basics — Diversification and Long-Term Investing¶

    If you want to build wealth rather than "trade" — this is much safer than forex. A parallel path.

    Forex vs Stocks vs ETF — The Key Difference¶

    Forex Stocks ETF
    What you buy Right to a price difference A share in a company A basket of stocks
    Dividends No Sometimes Often
    Horizon Days–weeks Months–years Years–decades
    Leverage 1:30–500 1:5 max 1:5 max
    Ruin risk High Medium Low
    Active management Constant Regular Minimal
    Suitable for beginners ❌ ⚠️ Experienced only ✅ Yes

    Main Advice: Start with ETFs¶

    ETF (Exchange Traded Fund) — a basket of dozens-to-thousands of stocks traded like a single share.

    Why: - ✅ Instant diversification (instead of 1 stock — 500) - ✅ Minimum effort — buy and hold - ✅ Low fees (0.03–0.5% per year) - ✅ Historical return of 7–10% per year (S&P 500 over 30 years) - ✅ Less emotion — no need to "pick stocks"

    The Most Reliable ETFs for Beginners¶

    Ticker What it is Share price Historical return
    VOO S&P 500 (500 largest US companies) ~$580 ~10%/year
    VTI Total US market ~$320 ~10%/year
    VXUS International stocks (non-US) ~$70 ~6%/year
    VT Total world market ~$130 ~8%/year
    QQQ NASDAQ-100 (technology) ~$540 ~14%/year (more volatile)
    BND US bonds ~$76 ~3%/year (defensive)

    Simple strategy (for beginners): - 70% VOO (US growth) - 20% VXUS (international diversification) - 10% BND (bonds = cushion)

    This is called a 3-fund portfolio. Simple and it works.

    How to Start Investing from Uzbekistan¶

    Option 1: International Broker¶

    Most popular: - Interactive Brokers (IBKR) — best in the world for serious investors, $0 minimum - eToro — user-friendly, has copy trading - Saxo Bank — high minimum, but top-tier - Freedom Finance — has an office in UZ

    Opening an IBKR account: 1. https://www.interactivebrokers.com 2. Registration (passport, address, proof of income) 3. Account opens within 3–7 days 4. Fund via SWIFT from a USD card in UZ 5. Buy VOO in one click

    Drawbacks: - KYC is complex (documents required in English) - Minimum $100 to trade - $10/month if balance is below $2,000 (waived in 2021, but verify)

    Option 2: Local Brokers¶

    In Uzbekistan: - UCSE — Uzbek stock exchange, local stocks - Avloda Capital — Uzbek broker - Eskhata Capital — based in Tajikistan but serves UZ

    Drawbacks: - Fewer instrument choices - Less liquidity - Often no direct access to S&P 500

    Option 3: Via a Crypto Exchange¶

    Some crypto exchanges offer tokenized stocks — representations of US stocks on the blockchain.

    Bybit, OKX — partially available Backed.fi — tokenized ETFs (including VOO as bVOO)

    Drawbacks: - Not true ownership — intermediary involved - Weak regulation - Counterparty risk

    Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) Strategy¶

    The core strategy of a passive investor:

    Once a month you buy VOO for a fixed amount. You do not try to "catch the bottom" or "exit at the top".

    January:   bought VOO for $200 at $570 → 0.35 shares
February:  bought for $200 at $590 → 0.34 shares
March:     bought for $200 at $545 (market fell) → 0.37 shares
April:     bought for $200 at $600 → 0.33 shares
May:       bought for $200 at $620 → 0.32 shares
...

Over 10 years → you have ~40 shares of VOO ≈ $25,000 (at 8%/year growth)

    The main rule: - ✅ Every month, on the same day - ✅ The same fixed amount - ✅ Never try to "predict" the market - ✅ Reinvest dividends

    Long-Term Math¶

    If you start investing $200/month in the S&P 500 at age 25:

    Years Amount invested Capital (at 8%/year)
    5 $12,000 $14,600
    10 $24,000 $36,500
    20 $48,000 $117,600
    30 $72,000 $283,100
    40 $96,000 $622,000

    $200/month → $622,000 over 40 years. That is the power of compound interest.

    Forex rarely delivers consistent results better than this, especially accounting for losses.

    When to CHOOSE Stocks Over Forex¶

    If you: - ✅ Want to simply accumulate capital - ✅ Are willing to hold for 10+ years - ✅ Do not want to actively spend time - ✅ Want less emotional stress - ✅ Are aged 25–40 (long time horizon)

    → Stocks/ETF is for you, not forex.

    When Forex Might Be Better¶

    If you: - ⚠️ Are willing to work actively every day - ⚠️ Have completed training and are psychologically resilient - ⚠️ Want additional income on top of investing - ⚠️ Accept high risk

    → Forex as a supplement, not a replacement for ETF.

    What NOT to Do¶

    ❌ Short-timeframe stocks trading (minutes, hours) - You compete against Wall Street HFT algorithms - Chances for a beginner are close to zero

    ❌ Buying individual stocks "on intuition" - 80% of active traders underperform the S&P 500 - Tesla? AMD? — you'll try to "guess" and end up losing

    ❌ Options / derivatives - Complex and risky - Most people lose money

    ❌ "Penny stocks" (stocks under $5) - 90% are scams (pump & dump) - Low liquidity

    Reading List for the Long-Term Investor¶

    1. "The Bogleheads' Guide to Investing" — Mel Lindauer
    2. "A Random Walk Down Wall Street" — Burton Malkiel
    3. "The Little Book of Common Sense Investing" — John Bogle
    4. "The Intelligent Investor" — Benjamin Graham (a classic)
    5. Podcasts: Animal Spirits, Bogleheads on Investing

    Strategy in One Line¶

    "Buy a broad ETF and hold it for 30 years. Don't open your terminal more than once a month."

    This is boring, but it works for 95% of people. Forex is a profession. ETF is a tool.

    What Is Better for You¶

    Do not choose either forex or ETF. The best strategy for most people:

    1. 80% of money → ETF (VOO + VXUS + BND) — long-term accumulation
    2. 15% of money → Free funds / emergency cushion
    3. 5% of money → Forex / crypto (if you really want to trade actively) — this is money you can afford to lose

    This way you guarantee long-term growth. And you still get the satisfaction of active trading.
