    FAQ — Beginner's Frequently Asked Questions¶

    30 typical questions that come up in the first few months. If you're in a panic or feeling uncertain — look here first.

    General¶

    1. How much money do I need to start?¶

    First, demo — the money there is virtual. Move to real with $100–$300, no more. This is an amount you can afford to lose. If $100 feels like "a lot" to you — it's too early to go live.

    2. How much can I earn per month?¶

    Realistically for an experienced, disciplined trader — 3–10% per month. That is already an impressive result. Any promises of "50% per month" are a scam.

    For a beginner, a realistic goal for the first year is to break even, losing as little as possible.

    3. Is Forex a scam or not?¶

    The forex market itself is real — all major banks and funds trade on it. Fraud does exist: - ❌ Unregulated brokers - ❌ "Gurus" selling signals - ❌ HYIP pyramids disguised as forex - ❌ Brokers that refuse to process withdrawals

    If you choose a regulated broker (FCA, CySEC, ASIC) and trade yourself — this is a legitimate activity.

    4. How many hours a day do I need to trade?¶

    It depends on your style. For H1/H4 (recommended for beginners), 30–60 minutes in the morning and evening for analysis and setup review is enough. Scalping requires 4–8 hours a day — not for beginners.

    5. Can I combine it with a full-time job?¶

    Yes, if you trade on H4/D1. You can open a trade in the morning and check it in the evening. Scalping + a job = financial ruin.

    6. Forex or stocks?¶

    • Stocks — for long-term investing, simpler and safer
    • Forex — for short-term speculation, more complex and riskier

    If the goal is to build capital → start with ETFs, then forex (if you want to).

    If the goal is to learn trading as a profession → forex.

    Technical¶

    7. Which broker is best for Uzbekistan?¶

    I cannot recommend a specific one — research it yourself. Criteria: 1. FCA / CySEC / ASIC regulation 2. At least 5+ years in the market 3. USDT TRC-20 for deposits 4. Russian-language support 5. Recent withdrawal reviews

    See forex-guide.md section 4.

    8. MT4 or MT5?¶

    MT5 — for a new account. Modern, with the best strategy tester. MT4 is supported for compatibility with older expert advisors.

    9. Which timeframe is best for a beginner?¶

    H1 and H4 — the sweet spot. Not M1/M5 (stress, noise), not D1/W1 (too few trades for learning).

    10. Which pairs should I start with?¶

    EUR/USD and GBP/USD. The highest liquidity, tight spreads, predictable behavior. No exotics (TRY, ZAR, MXN) — there you can get a 5% gap against you in a single day.

    11. What leverage should I use?¶

    1:30 or 1:10, even if the broker offers 1:500. High leverage is an available option, not a mandatory one.

    12. What is a Margin account vs ECN vs Cent?¶

    • Cent — the deposit is counted in cents: $10 = 1,000 cents. Suitable for practicing with minimal amounts (but not instead of demo!).
    • Standard — a regular account. Minimum $100–$500.
    • ECN — direct market access. Tight spread + commission. For experienced traders.

    13. Which indicators should I use?¶

    EMA 50, EMA 200, RSI(14). That's enough for a year. Don't pile on indicators — 7 indicators paralyze decision-making.

    Trades¶

    14. What should I do if my trade goes into the negative?¶

    Nothing. Sit and wait for the stop or take-profit. Do not move the stop further, do not average down, do not close manually.

    15. What should I do if my trade is well into profit?¶

    If your strategy rules say so — wait for TP. You can move the stop to breakeven (entry price) after the trade has gained 1R.

    16. What should I do if my stop was hit and then price reversed in my direction?¶

    Nothing. This is normal. Stops get hit in 40–60% of cases in any system. If stops always triggered "just before the reversal" — everyone would be profitable.

    17. I opened a trade without a stop-loss and lost a lot. What should I do?¶

    Remember this forever: never trade without a stop. Right now: 1. Close the position manually immediately, even at a loss 2. Write it in your journal as a "rule violation" 3. Take a 1–3 day break

    18. Can I trade several pairs at the same time?¶

    You can, but for beginners — 1-2 pairs. Account for correlation: EUR/USD and GBP/USD are highly correlated → if you open both long, that is effectively doubled risk.

    19. What if my internet or laptop disconnects during a trade?¶

    If you have a stop-loss — no problem, it will trigger on the broker's side. If there is no SL — that is your catastrophic mistake. That is why you always set a SL.

    20. How do I close a trade before take-profit if the market reverses?¶

    Open MT5 → right-click on the trade → Close Order → confirm. But by your strategy rules you should wait for SL or TP. Closing "by intuition" is the path to losses.

    Psychology¶

    21. I had a series of 5 consecutive losses. Is the strategy bad?¶

    Not necessarily. With a 50% win rate and R:R 1:2, the probability of 5 consecutive losses = 3.1%. This happens roughly once every 32 trades. That is normal.

    The strategy has a problem if: - The win rate dropped by 20+% relative to the backtest - Profit Factor < 1.0 over 30+ trades - The drawdown exceeded the historical maximum by 2x

    Solution: pause, figure it out, do not change parameters mid-flight.

    22. I cannot handle fear. What should I do?¶

    • Reduce your position size by 2-3x. If you are scared — the position is too large.
    • Remember: every trade = 1R of risk. Think of it as "a small lottery ticket."
    • 100 trades give you statistics. 1 trade means nothing.

    23. It annoys me when others close early and I hold. Is that normal?¶

    That is exactly discipline. If you follow the rules, what others do should not matter to you. If it does matter — you don't trust your system yet.

    24. I constantly check my phone while trading¶

    Install an app blocker on your phone for 2 hours while you work. If you can't — leave your phone in another room.

    Money¶

    25. How do I withdraw money from a broker to Uzbekistan?¶

    The simplest route: broker → USDT (TRC-20) → exchange (Binance / Bybit) → P2P into soum. Details: see uz/withdrawal-guide.md.

    26. Do I need to pay taxes on trading profits?¶

    Yes, individuals must file declarations. For the exact rates and forms, check soliq.uz or consult an accountant. Keep your broker statements — you will need them.

    27. Can I trade with borrowed / credit money?¶

    Absolutely not. Psychological pressure = catastrophe. Only use money you can afford to lose.

    Education¶

    28. Where do I start from scratch?¶

    1. Read forex-guide.md (twice)
    2. Open a demo account with a regulated broker
    3. Trade 30 demo trades using one strategy
    4. Analyze mistakes using a journal
    5. Trade another 30 trades
    6. After 3 months of consistent profit → go live with $100–$300

    29. How long does it take to become profitable?¶

    Realistically — 1–3 years to reach stability. Many people quit earlier. Do not quit, but do not rush to go live either.

    30. What should I read after this guide?¶

    1. BabyPips "School of Pipsology" — free course (English)
    2. Alexander Elder "Trading for a Living"
    3. Mark Douglas "The Disciplined Trader"
    4. John Murphy "Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets"

    Ask me. I answered 30 typical ones, but there are many more questions. Any situation where you are unsure — it is better to ask than to make a mistake.
