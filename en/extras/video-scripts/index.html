    Video Scripts for Self-Recording¶

    10 ready-made video scripts, 5–15 minutes each. Record them yourself on your phone — it's the best way to memorise the material and test your understanding. Re-watch your own videos in a year and see how much you've grown.

    How to Use¶

    1. Read the script
    2. Make 3 recording attempts — each one better than the last
    3. Don't publish — this is for yourself
    4. Re-watch your video after 3–6 months
    5. Re-record once you know more

    Why recording works: your brain remembers information you explained out loud far better than anything you simply read.

    Video 1: "What Is Forex and Why 80% Lose" — 8 minutes¶

    Goal: test whether you truly understand the BASICS.

    Structure¶

    Introduction (1 min):

    "Hi, my name is [name]. I've been studying forex for [N] weeks/months. Today I'll explain what it is and why most people lose money here."

    What is forex (2 min): - Market volume: $7.5 trillion per day - Who trades: banks, funds, corporations, and… us (5%) - How people profit: exchange-rate differences - Show an EUR/USD chart and explain what "buy" and "sell" mean

    Why most people lose (3 min): - Emotions - High leverage - No risk management - Following "gurus" on Telegram - Chasing quick money

    What you should do (2 min): - At least 3 months on a demo account - A journal entry for every trade - Risk 0.5–1% per trade - Discipline matters more than intuition

    Video 2: "Stop-Loss — My Contract with Myself" — 6 minutes¶

    Goal: explain SL in a way you'll never forget.

    Structure¶

    • What a stop-loss is
    • Why it's mandatory in every trade
    • Where to place it correctly (behind a swing + 5 pips)
    • Demo: show 3 trades on a chart with different stops
    • What happens without SL: one sharp spike = account blown
    • Key idea: "If I've already thought about moving the stop — I've already lost."

    Video 3: "Trading Psychology — My Biggest Enemy" — 10 minutes¶

    Goal: record your understanding of psychology before your first live trade.

    Structure¶

    • Tilt: what it is and why it's dangerous
    • FOMO: examples from my own life
    • Greed: "I'll close my profit early"
    • Fear: "Better not to open at all"
    • How I deal with all of this:
    • A physical checklist
    • A journal entry for every trade
    • 3-loss limit → stop for the day
    • Quote: "Discipline beats intuition"

    Video 4: "My First Trade — A Full Breakdown" — 5 minutes¶

    Goal: break down ONE trade in full detail.

    Structure¶

    • Which pair
    • Screenshot of the chart before entry
    • Why I entered (5-point checklist)
    • Where SL and TP are, and why exactly there
    • Position size calculation
    • How it closed
    • What I learned from this trade

    Best format: record on the day of the trade. Take your time with the analysis.

    Video 5: "My Trading Plan — What I'm Accountable For" — 12 minutes¶

    Goal: say your plan out loud — make it stick.

    Structure¶

    Work through the sections of your trading-plan-template.md and talk through: - Who I am as a trader - How much I invest - What I trade and when - My limits - My entry rules - What I will NEVER do

    At the end:

    "I commit to following this plan. If I break it — I take a one-week pause."

    Video 6: "Technical Analysis — My Way" — 15 minutes¶

    Goal: show that you understand candles and indicators.

    Structure¶

    Open an EUR/USD H1 chart. Explain: - What a candle is (OHLC) - Which patterns I know (hammer, engulfing, doji) - What a trend is - What support/resistance is - EMA 50 / EMA 200 — what they show - RSI — what it's for

    Show real examples on the chart for each concept. Not theory — point to actual candles.

    Video 7: "My Strategy in One Line" — 7 minutes¶

    Goal: be able to explain your strategy to a complete beginner in 5 minutes.

    Structure¶

    If you can't explain it — you don't understand it yourself.

    "My strategy is a breakout / pullback / moving-average crossover."

    Cover: - When I go long - When I go short - Where the stop is - Where the target is - Risk/Reward - When I do NOT trade (news, Friday, tilt)

    Test: show this video to your mum or a friend. If they understood it — you understand it.

    Video 8: "Lesson of the Month — My Mistakes" — 8 minutes¶

    Goal: a monthly report to yourself.

    Structure¶

    • How many trades this month
    • Win rate
    • Profit Factor
    • Top 3 mistakes of the month
    • One lesson for next month
    • Any change to the strategy (if any)

    Record once a month. After a year — 12 videos of self-reflection.

    Video 9: "Why I'm NOT Trading Right Now" — 5 minutes¶

    Goal: explain the situations when you sit out.

    Structure¶

    This is the opposite of "when I trade." Cover: - Didn't get enough sleep - Emotional stress - High-impact news - 3 losses in a row - Strong range / chop - Friday evening - When I'm not confident in the setup

    Key idea: "Better to skip — than to lose."

    Video 10: "One Year Later — What I've Learned" — 20 minutes¶

    Goal: a yearly report to yourself.

    Structure¶

    • Where I was a year ago (zero)
    • What I did: books, demo, journal
    • Total number of trades for the year
    • Final statistics
    • 5 key lessons
    • Where I am now (live? demo?)
    • Plan for the next year

    Record after one year. It will be incredible to watch back.

    Recording Tips¶

    Simple Setup¶

    • Phone on a tripod ($10 on AliExpress)
    • Good lighting (window behind the camera)
    • Quiet room
    • Chart open on a laptop/screen behind you

    Screen Recording Software¶

    • OBS Studio (free, open source)
    • Loom (paid, simple)
    • QuickTime (on Mac, free)
    • iPhone screen recording (for mobile MT5)

    Structure for Every Video¶

    1. Introduction (15 sec): "Here's what I'll cover"
    2. Content (the main bulk)
    3. Key takeaway (1 min)
    4. What I'll do with this knowledge (30 sec)

    Not for Publishing¶

    These videos are only for you. Don't upload them to YouTube — trading content there is full of people who understand nothing but shout very loudly.

    When in 2 years you're consistently profitable, you can think about starting a channel with real trade breakdowns. But not before then. Until that point — this is for yourself.

    Bonus: The 5-Minute "Explain It to Your Partner" Exercise¶

    Once a week, explain something from trading to your partner, spouse, or parent in 5 minutes: - What EMA is - Why risk:reward matters - What tilt is

    If they understood — you understand. If they didn't — you don't. This is the ultimate test.
