    Broker Comparison — A Guide to Choosing¶

    ⚠️ Important: This is not an advertisement or recommendation. Specific conditions change — check the broker's website yourself before registering. I receive no commission from any broker.

    Disclaimer¶

    • Conditions (spreads, minimum deposit, regulation, bonuses) change. This information was current at the time of writing.
    • A broker may have different legal entities in the same country: for example, FxPro UK Limited (FCA) vs FxPro Global Markets (SCB, Bahamas).
    • Clients from Uzbekistan are often offered an offshore subsidiary with higher leverage but less protection. That is your choice: more leverage vs more protection.

    Evaluation Criteria¶

    Criterion What is good

    EUR/USD spread 0.5–2.0 pips
    Accepts clients from Uzbekistan Yes

    Top-5 in Detail (for clients from Uzbekistan)¶

    IC Markets¶

    Pros: - ECN account with one of the tightest spreads in the industry (0.0 pips on EUR/USD + $3.5 commission per standard lot) - ASIC and CySEC regulation - cTrader support (in addition to MT4/MT5)

    Cons: - Minimum $200 (not critical) - Global subsidiary — Seychelles (for CIS clients)

    Best for: experienced traders for whom tight spreads matter most.

    Pepperstone¶

    Pros: - FCA + ASIC + CySEC regulation simultaneously - High-quality execution - Razor account from 0.0 pips + $3.5 commission

    Cons: - Minimum $200 - For CIS clients — Bahamas subsidiary

    Best for: those seeking the balance between regulation and tight spreads.

    Exness¶

    Pros: - Very low minimum deposit ($10) - Instant withdrawals (often within minutes) - USDT support - One of the most popular brokers in the CIS

    Cons: - CySEC regulation only for European clients. For CIS — Seychelles (FSA) - Extremely high leverage (up to 1:Unlimited) — a temptation for beginners

    Best for: beginners starting with a small amount. But do not use high leverage.

    Tickmill¶

    Pros: - FCA + CySEC regulation - Pro account from 0.0 pips + $2 commission — a direct rival to IC Markets - Transparent reporting

    Cons: - Minimum $100 - For CIS — Seychelles

    Best for: beginners who want reliable regulation combined with low spreads.

    FxPro¶

    Pros: - 19 years on the market - FCA + CySEC + DFSA - cTrader, MT4, MT5 support - Good Russian-language customer support

    Cons: - Spreads higher than ECN brokers (1.2 pips) - Minimum $100

    Best for: those seeking stability and a long track record.

    How to Check a Broker in 5 Minutes¶

    Step 1: License¶

    1. Go to the regulator's website:
    2. FCA UK: register.fca.org.uk
    3. CySEC: cysec.gov.cy/en-GB/entities/investment-firms/cypriot/
    4. ASIC: asic.gov.au
    5. Search for the broker by name
    6. Check whether the license is active and what services it covers

    Step 2: Reviews¶

    1. ForexPeaceArmy.com — search for the broker, look at reviews from the last 6 months
    2. TrustPilot — filter by year
    3. Smart-Lab and mql5.com — Russian-language reviews

    Step 3: Real Test¶

    1. Open a demo account (no money required) → try the interface
    2. Contact support — ask a question, check the response time
    3. Do not deposit a large amount right away — start with $100–$300 on a live account

    Step 4: Withdrawal¶

    1. After funding, immediately try to withdraw $20–$30
    2. If the withdrawal goes through in 1–3 days without issues → the broker can be trusted
    3. If they delayed it or asked for unusual documents → close the account

    Red Flags — Do NOT Open an Account¶

    🚩 "Guaranteed profit of 30% per month" 🚩 "EUR is at a record high right now — deposit and get a 100% bonus!" 🚩 They call and pressure you to increase your deposit 🚩 "A personal manager will trade on your behalf" 🚩 No license anywhere, or only in Vanuatu / SVG 🚩 The site has been running for 2 years and there is nothing about the company on Google 🚩 A bonus that requires trading 30 lots in one week 🚩 Deposit withdrawal is blocked until the bonus wagering requirement is met 🚩 Support only responds during business hours on weekdays (once a week)

    Typical Fraud Schemes¶

    1. Bottom-level "Forex Investment" Schemes¶

    You are promised training in trading in exchange for a share of the profit. In reality: - They ask you to transfer funds to their "account" - They show you "successful trades" (photoshopped) - After a month, all contact is cut off

    2. Fake Platforms¶

    You are given an interface that looks like MT5, but it is a simulator. The money has gone to fraudsters; the quotes are fake.

    3. "Exclusive Signal Channel on Telegram"¶

    You pay $200/month for signals. Out of 100 subscribers: - 30 "enter profitably" — they receive one set of signals - 30 "enter at a loss" — they receive a different set - After a month, 30 praise the channel, 30 leave, the rest say nothing - The channel looks profitable, but this is a statistical illusion

    4. PAMM Accounts with a Guarantee¶

    "Trust your money to a manager." After 3 months: - 70% of the capital is gone - "The market fell, it is not our fault" - You will not get your money back

    If You Have Already Been Scammed¶

    1. Stop all transfers immediately
    2. Collect all documents (statements, screenshots, correspondence)
    3. File a complaint with the regulator (if the broker is regulated)
    4. File a report with local law enforcement (if there is a criminal case)
    5. On a crypto exchange (if money was sent via USDT) — freeze your wallet and contact their support
    6. Do not pay "money recovery specialists" — this is a second wave of fraud

    Final Advice¶

    It is better to spend a week choosing a broker than a month recovering from a loss.

    The top-5 brokers listed above have stood the test of time. Any one of them is MANY TIMES safer than some "new young broker with a 200% bonus."
