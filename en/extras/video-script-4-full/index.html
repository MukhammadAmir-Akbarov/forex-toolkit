    Video Script #4: "Breaking Down My First Trade"¶

    Full on-camera text — ~5–7 minutes. Record this immediately after your first intentional demo trade, while the emotions are still fresh.

    🎬 TIMECODES¶

    • 0:00–0:30 — Introduction
    • 0:30–2:30 — Context: what I saw before the trade
    • 2:30–4:00 — Trade parameters
    • 4:00–5:30 — How it developed and closed
    • 5:30–7:00 — The main lesson

    ⚠️ BEFORE RECORDING¶

    ☐ Chart screenshot BEFORE entry saved
☐ Chart screenshot AFTER close saved
☐ Trade journal fully filled in
☐ Recording on the day of the trade, not a week later
☐ At least 30 minutes have passed since close (emotions have settled)

    🎥 SCRIPT¶

    [0:00 — OPENING]¶

    Hi. Today I'm doing a breakdown of my first intentional trade on a demo account. This is trade number [N] in my journal. Not the very first trade of my life — but the first one where I followed the plan from start to finish.

    The goal of this video is not to show a profit or a loss, but to analyse the process. In a year I'll rewatch this video and see how I was thinking at the beginning of the journey.

    [show the "before" chart screenshot]

    [0:30 — CONTEXT]¶

    Trade date: [date]. Time: [time].

    Pair: EUR/USD, entry timeframe H1, context reviewed on H4 and D1.

    What I saw on the chart:

    On D1 — an uptrend, price above EMA200, making higher highs and higher lows for the last [N] days.

    On H4 — price pulled back to a support zone around [level]. The market had already bounced from this zone 2 times over the previous [period].

    On H1 — a candle appeared that touched EMA50 from below and closed as a bullish engulfing. The body of the green candle completely covered the body of the previous red candle.

    [highlight the circled elements on the screenshot]

    RSI(14) at the time of entry was [value] — neither overbought nor oversold. This matches my filter: RSI between 40 and 65.

    I checked the Forex Factory calendar — there were no red-impact news events in the next 2 hours. Day of the week — [not Friday evening].

    I ran through the checklist — all 10 points confirmed.

    [2:30 — TRADE PARAMETERS]¶

    Parameters:

    • Entry price: [number] — close of the signal candle on H1
    • Stop Loss: [number] — below the signal candle's low plus 5 pips
    • Take Profit: [number] — 2× the distance from entry to stop

    Stop in pips: [N] Target in pips: [2×N] R:R: 1 to 2 — this is the minimum I allow myself.

    I calculated position size using position_calculator.py: - Deposit: [N dollars] - Risk: 0.5% — that is [N dollars] in cash terms - With a stop of [N pips] on EUR/USD — position size is [N] lots

    [show the calculator screenshot]

    I opened the trade in MT5: one press of F9, entered the lot size, SL and TP in the order window right away — this is the most important step.

    [4:00 — DEVELOPMENT AND CLOSE]¶

    After opening I closed MT5 and went about my business. This is important. If I sit and watch every single candle — I'll start second-guessing myself and want to close manually.

    After [N hours] I checked — the trade is [in profit / in loss].

    [Option A: trade is profitable] Price moved [N pips] in my direction and the take-profit triggered. The trade closed automatically. Profit: [N dollars] or +2R.

    [Option B: trade is a loss] Price pulled back to the stop and hit it. Loss: [N dollars] or −1R. I knew about this possibility before entering — which is why I'm calm.

    The key point — I did not interfere. I didn't move the stop. I didn't close manually. I didn't open a "revenge trade."

    [show the annotated "after" chart screenshot]

    [5:30 — THE MAIN LESSON]¶

    What did I learn from this trade?

    One. The checklist works. When I physically ticked all 10 boxes — I had complete confidence in the entry. No "what if I missed something."

    Two. The position calculator is non-negotiable. I would have set a volume 3–4× larger by eye. In that case the loss would have been 2–3% of the deposit instead of 0.5%.

    Three. The hardest part is not pressing the button, but waiting. The trade ran for [N hours]. During that time I 3 times thought "I'll close it for a small profit / loss." But I held.

    Four. The feeling of "rightness" from a successful trade executed to plan is far stronger than the feeling of a random lucky win. I trust myself a little more now.

    [pause]

    What will I change in the next trade? - [specific point] — for example, check DXY correlations before entry - [specific point] — for example, note the time of day in the journal

    [to camera]

    After 30 trades I'll rewatch this video and see what has changed in my thinking. Thanks for watching.

    [end]

    📝 PLACEHOLDER REPLACEMENT TEMPLATE¶

    Before recording, replace everything [in square brackets] with your real data:

    [name]               → your name
[N] weeks/months     → actual duration of study
[date]               → trade date
[time]               → entry time in UTC+5
[level]              → support/resistance value
[value]              → RSI(14) at that moment
[number] (entry)     → exact entry price
[number] (SL)        → exact stop price
[number] (TP)        → exact take-profit price
[N pips]             → distance to stop
[N dollars]          → deposit / risk / result
[N lots]             → position size
[N hours]            → how many hours the trade was open

    🎯 CLOSING OPTIONS¶

    If the trade is profitable:

    This does not mean I've learned. One profitable trade is the statistics of a single coin flip. A series of 30+ will show whether I actually have an edge.

    If the trade is a loss:

    A loss by the plan is a success. I followed the rules; price simply moved against me. This is not my mistake — it is part of the statistics. Over the long run with R:R 1:2 I come out ahead.

    If I closed manually before SL/TP:

    That was a mistake. The plan was to wait for the stop or the take-profit. I didn't wait. I logged it in the journal under "rule violations." Next time — don't do that.

    🔁 WHAT TO DO IN A MONTH¶

    Rewatch this video after 30 trades or after 1 month:

    ☐ What was NAIVE in my analysis?
☐ What did I do WELL?
☐ What am I doing DIFFERENTLY now?
☐ Which terms did I poorly understand back then?
☐ Am I calmer in trades now?

    This is exactly feedback from your past self to your current self. Priceless.
